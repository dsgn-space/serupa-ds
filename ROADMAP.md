# Roadmap

Where Serupa is going, by theme, not by date: the order can change as products tell us what they need. What has shipped is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md), and what counts as a breaking change is in the README's [Versioning](README.md#versioning) section.

## Now

- **Polish before breadth.** Every component, pattern and docs page held to the same details: sizes, states, words and pictures that agree across the whole system. The bug queue always goes first.
- **The next major.** Breaking changes are held for one scheduled major at a time and proposed first in [docs/rfcs/](docs/rfcs/), with `npx serupa-migrate` handling the renames.

## Next

- **Deeper components.** The features products ask for again and again, one component at a time.
- **More charts.** More kinds on the same tokens, themes and accessibility rules, with the numbers available as text.
- **More page patterns.** New whole screens when a product asks for one, as examples to copy and as tests that the components work together.

A major comes only when something has to stop working, and is written up for comment first, with a migration guide.
