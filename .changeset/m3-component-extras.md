Three small additions from the Material Design 3 study. `BottomNavLink` takes a `badge`, shown on the icon's top-end corner and read after the link's name. `Fab` takes `extended="auto"`: it folds to its icon while the page scrolls down and shows its label again on the way up or at the top. `TagGroup` items take a decorative `icon` or `avatar` before the label.
