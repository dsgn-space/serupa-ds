Switch: off, the track is the light `track` gray in place of `border-control`, and the thumb has the small `elevation-part` shadow (on or off) and no edge, as Apple's. On the light track the off switch is under 3:1, a design choice. New token `--switch-thumb-shadow`; `--switch-track` now defaults to `track`, `--switch-track-hover` to `border-strong` and `--switch-track-pressed` to `border-control`.
