Switch: off, the track is the light `track` gray in place of `border-control`, and the thumb has a 1px `border` edge and the small `elevation-part` shadow, as Apple's (on or off). On the light track the off switch is under 3:1, a design choice. New tokens `--switch-thumb-border`, `--switch-thumb-border-hover` and `--switch-thumb-shadow`; `--switch-track` now defaults to `track`, `--switch-track-hover` to `border-strong` and `--switch-track-pressed` to `border-control`.
