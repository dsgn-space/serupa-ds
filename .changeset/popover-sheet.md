Added (Popover): `sheet="mobile"` shows it under 640px as a sheet along the foot of the screen, the screen's width, with rounded top corners; it rises from below and follows the screen while open. `sheet` makes it a sheet everywhere. It stays the same non-modal dialog, with the same keys and focus. Two tokens size it: `--popover-sheet-radius` and `--popover-sheet-gap`.
