Added: Menubar (beta) and MenubarMenu, an application's menu bar (File, Edit, View) in the WAI-ARIA menubar pattern: one Tab stop for the bar, ← → across its menus, ↓ or Enter to open one, and ← → in an open menu to move straight to the next menu, open. Pointing at another name while a menu is open opens that one. Each menu is a Menu, so its items, submenus, shortcuts and type-ahead work as they do there.
