Switch: `icon` puts a check on the thumb while the switch is on, so on and off differ by shape as well as by color (new token `--switch-icon`). Slider: `orientation="vertical"` runs the track bottom to top, with min at the bottom, `--slider-length` long (new token, 192px); Up and Right raise it in either direction of text.
