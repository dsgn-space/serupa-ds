- Added: Kanban (beta), a board of columns of cards. Cards move by dragging a handle (the board and columns scroll near their edges), by keyboard (Space to pick up, arrows within and across columns, mirrored right to left, Space to drop, Escape to cancel) or from each card's "Move to…" menu, and each step is announced ("Fix login moved to Done, position 2 of 5"). Columns show a count, with an optional limit that says "Over limit" in words. With 17 component tokens.
