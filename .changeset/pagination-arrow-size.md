Pagination: the arrows are the size of the icon in an icon-only Button of the same size, 14, 16 and 20px. They were 16, 20 and 20px, a step heavier than the Select or Button beside them, with no step from md to lg. Three new tokens set them: `--pagination-icon-size-sm`, `--pagination-icon-size-md` and `--pagination-icon-size-lg`.
