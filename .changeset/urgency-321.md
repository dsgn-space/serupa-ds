Only danger interrupts. An Alert with `tone="warning"` is now `role="status"`, read out at the next pause as a warning toast is, instead of `role="alert"`; pass `role="alert"` for a warning that must interrupt. A SaveStatus error is now said assertively, as a danger toast is, since the work isn't saved.
