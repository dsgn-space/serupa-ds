Calendar and DatePicker: the heading opens the year's months, and theirs twelve years, to jump to a far date (a date of birth) in a few clicks or keys; Escape steps back a level. The weeks sit 4px apart (`--calendar-row-gap`), and a range is one square band per week with its ends rounded on the outer side only. DateRangePicker's two dates are as wide as their text, so the dash has the same gap on each side. Menus, popovers, tooltips and pickers hide while the field or button they hang from is scrolled out of view, rather than staying pinned to the window's edge.
