Five new patterns to copy: Job application, Event registration, Saved items, Quiz and Accept invite. The job application lists the job's facts before a short form whose CV says what it takes, and focuses the answer with what happens next. Event registration shows times in the event's own time zone, places left as a meter with words, works out the total as tickets are chosen, offers a calendar file, and becomes a waiting list once full. Saved items says what changed since saving in words, names each button by its item, and removes with Undo. The quiz makes each question a fieldset, uses checkboxes for "Choose all that apply", and once checked says right or not in words with why. Accept invite says who invited you in the heading and which account you'd join with before Join, and an ended invite says why.
