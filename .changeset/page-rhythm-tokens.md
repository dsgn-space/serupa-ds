Two spacing roles for the two page rhythms: `space-page` (24px), for the page header, sections and cards of an app screen, and `space-band` (64px), for the bands of a long-form or marketing page and the space after its page header. `space-section` (32px) now describes the sections of a long-form page. The foundations' Spacing rhythm table gives both rhythms side by side. App screens were documented at 48–64px from header to content, while every pattern used 24px.
