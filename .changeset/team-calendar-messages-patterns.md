Two new patterns for working together. Team calendar: a team's events in EventCalendar, added from a form that says what's missing, moved by dragging or by keyboard and saved as they move (a move that can't be saved goes back and says so), and opened in a dialog that says when the event is in words and deletes it with Undo. Messages: one-to-one conversations beside the open one, each row named by the person with unread in words, nothing marked read before it's opened, and a message that can't be sent marked "Not sent" with Try again.
