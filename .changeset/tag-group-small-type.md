TagGroup: a tag sets its words as a small Button does, 12px on a 16px line in its 24px (was 14px), with 14px icons, the check and the × (`--tag-group-icon-size`, was 16px). An avatar is 20px and sits inside the tag's border with 1px clear of it (`--tag-group-avatar-size`, was 24px, which covered the border). A plain tag's border is the light `border-strong` a field and an outline Badge have (`--tag-group-border`, was `border-control`); a picked tag keeps its 3:1 brand border.
