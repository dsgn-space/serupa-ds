Dark themes fill with their light theme's own brand shade, and brand text has its own token. A primary button, a checked box, the selected day, a Switch that's on and a Progress bar are the same color in light and dark, under white text in both: in Blue Dark the fill was `blue-400` (#60a5fa) with `blue-950` text, and is now #2968f0 with white text, `blue-600` (#2563eb) lightened only as far as 3:1 on a raised dark card asks. Links, icons, the line under a tab and outline buttons in dark keep the lighter shade they had, as the new `primary-text`.

- Added: `primary-text` and `primary-text-hover`, the brand as text, a line, a border or an icon: the same shade as `primary` in light themes, brand 400 and 300 in dark (what `primary` and `primary-hover` were), 4.5:1 on every surface and tint. With them the `text-primary-text`, `border-primary-text` and other utility classes.
- Changed: in dark themes `primary`, `primary-hover` and `primary-pressed` are the light theme's shades, each a little lighter (no brand moves more than 0.07 of OKLCH lightness), and `on-primary` is white. Hover and held down go darker in dark themes too, as in light. With more contrast a dark theme keeps brand 300 under brand-950 text. Yellow Dark is unchanged: the bright `yellow-400` under `yellow-950` text.
- Changed: Yellow Light fills with the bright `yellow-500` under `yellow-950` text (it was `yellow-700` under white, which read as brown), with hover #d9a500 and held down `yellow-600`.
- Added: `primary-edge`, a 1px edge just inside a `primary` fill that has no text on it, for a theme whose fill is under 3:1 on the page: `yellow-700` in Yellow Light (`yellow-500` is 1.9:1 on white), transparent in every other theme. Checkbox, Radio, Switch, Slider, Progress, Meter, TreeView, Stepper, Calendar, PeriodPicker, Pagination, pill Tabs, Carousel, Timeline and NotificationCenter draw it. Progress's ring has a second arc under the first for it. Each draws it through its own component token (`--checkbox-edge-checked`, `--switch-track-edge-checked`, `--progress-fill-edge` and so on).
- Added: `--switch-pending-checked`, the loading ring on an on Switch's thumb. `--switch-pending` is now the ring on the off thumb only, and defaults to `primary-text`.
- Changed: every component token for brand text, an icon, a line or a border points at `primary-text` (Link, Button's secondary and link variants, Tabs' line, Badge's subtle and outline, ToggleButton, TagGroup, the checks in Select, Combobox and TimePicker: 51 tokens in 34 components); the fills keep `primary`. Bare links and the text caret take `primary-text`.
- Changed (serupa-brand): a brand's dark theme is made the same way: its fill is the light theme's, lightened as far as 3:1 on a raised card asks, with white text, and `primary-text` is the shade that passes as text.

- **What breaks:** CSS that sets text from `--primary` (or uses `text-primary`) is the fill's shade in dark themes, about 3.6:1 on a dark card instead of 7:1. A theme made by `serupa-brand` before 18.0.0, or written by hand, sets `--primary` and not `--primary-text`, so its links and outline buttons draw in blue until it's made again. Code that counted on `--on-primary` being dark in dark themes (an icon drawn on a custom brand-400 fill) gets white. In Yellow Light, text on `primary` is dark, and a custom mark filled with `primary` (no text on it) is 1.9:1 on white without an edge.
- **Why:** a dark theme's brand color was two or three palette steps lighter than the light theme's, with the text on it flipped from white to dark, so the two modes read as different brands. One token did two jobs with different needs: a fill has to carry white text (a mid shade), and text on a dark page has to be light. Danger already worked this way, `red-600` under white in both modes with `danger-text` for text.
- **Migration:** `serupa-migrate --from 17` turns `color: var(--primary)` into `color: var(--primary-text)` (and `-hover`), and the `text-primary` classes into `text-primary-text`; it lists each theme file that sets `--primary` without `--primary-text` (`serupa-migrate` makes it). Make a `serupa-brand` theme again with the same command (`npx serupa-brand acme "#0f766e" -o src/acme-theme.css`). In a hand-written theme, set `--primary-text` and `--primary-text-hover` to what `--primary` and `--primary-hover` were, and in dark give `--primary` the light theme's shade with `--on-primary: var(--white)`. A page that set `--switch-pending` for an on Switch sets `--switch-pending-checked` too. Give a custom mark filled with `primary` the edge: `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--primary-edge)`.
