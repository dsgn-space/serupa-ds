Added: SplitButton, a main action joined to an arrow button that opens a menu of other ways to do it (Publish, with Schedule and Save as draft). The arrow is named by `menuLabel` and gets `aria-haspopup` and `aria-expanded` from Menu; `variant`, `size`, `destructive`, `disabled` and `loading` work as on Button.
