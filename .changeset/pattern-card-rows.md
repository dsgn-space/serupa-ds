Cards in the Billing, Checkout and Onboarding patterns that are meant to be rows now lay out as rows. Billing's plan and payment method, Checkout's review parts, and Onboarding's hidden checklist were stacked and centred, because Card is a column. Each now sits at the start, with its button or link beside it, and wraps under it on a narrow screen.
