Button and Badge take `loadingLabel` for the words said to screen readers when loading starts, in place of `loadingText`, which still works and is deprecated (removed in 15.0.0). A prop named `…Text` is shown on screen (Combobox's `loadingText`, Button's `successText`); one named `…Label` is said, as Chart's, DataGrid's and Kanban's `loadingLabel` already were.
