Added: RangeCalendar, a calendar that stays on the page and picks a start and an end (a booking's dates, a report's period), with one or two months side by side. The first pick is one end and the second the other, in either order; the days between preview as the pointer or focus moves, and the value changes once both are picked. The ends are named "start date" and "end date" and the days between "in the range", the range is said once it's picked, and Escape drops a half-picked range.
