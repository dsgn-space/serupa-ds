Added: Countdown, the time left until a deadline as a clock ("4:59") or in words ("4 minutes, 59 seconds"). It's a `<time role="timer">` named by its label. It says the time aloud at a few steps and when it ends, never every second. It keeps its own clock from `to`, or shows `seconds` when the app keeps the clock.
