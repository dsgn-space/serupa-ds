Added: PeriodPicker (beta), to pick a month, a quarter or a year from a grid in a popup, for a report's month, a billing period or a dashboard's quarter. The button reads the period in words ("November 2026", "Q4 2026"), and a form gets it as `2026-11`, `2026-Q4` or `2026`. It takes `min` and `max`, the locale's month names and digits, and the keys of Calendar's grid.
