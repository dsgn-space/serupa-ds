Button, IconButton, Link and Divider take `onMedia="dark" | "light"` for use over a photo, a video or a brand-colored panel. Over dark media they draw in white, over light media in black, the same in all six themes, with a translucent hover wash, a focus ring in the same ink and no shadow. New component tokens: `--button-on-dark`, `--button-on-dark-text`, `--button-on-light`, `--button-on-light-text`, `--link-on-dark`, `--link-on-light`, `--divider-on-dark` and `--divider-on-light`.
