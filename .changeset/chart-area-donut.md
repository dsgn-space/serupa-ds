- Added (Chart): `type="area"`, a line with the space under it filled down to zero, and `stacked`, which stands each area on the one before it so the top line is the total. A point still says its own value.
- Added (Chart): `type="donut"`, the first series' values above zero as slices of a ring, with the total in the middle. Each slice is named with its value and share ("Search: 4,200 (42%)"), the keyboard goes round them, the legend names each one with its share, and slices after the first carry the bar patterns. Past seven categories the rest are one "Other" slice, so it keeps to the eight chart colors. "Show as table" lists every category.
- Added (labels): `chartOther`, `chartShare` and `chartTotal`, and `chartKind` words for area and donut charts, in all 20 language packs.
