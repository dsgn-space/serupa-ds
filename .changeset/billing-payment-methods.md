The Billing pattern shows any way a plan is paid, not only a card. Pass `paymentMethod` for a direct debit from a bank account or an e-wallet, and the link beside it reads Change payment method. A card that runs out before the next payment shows an "Expires soon" badge and says so in words. With `paymentMethod={null}`, the page says nothing is on file yet, by when to add one, and offers Add payment method.
