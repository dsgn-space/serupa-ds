Added: MentionInput (beta), a multi-line text field where typing "@" opens a list of people to mention at the caret. Arrow keys, Enter, Tab or a click put "@Name" into the text, the number of matches is said politely, and Escape closes the list without closing the dialog the field is in. `mentionsIn(text, options)` lists who a text mentions, so the app can notify them. CONTRIBUTING now says when a component leaves beta.
