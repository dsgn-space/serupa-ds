Typography: set `--font-heading` to use your own face for headings. The display and heading roles (`type-display`, `type-heading-1` to `-4`), Heading, and the Dialog and Drawer titles try it first, then the language's sans stack, so Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic and other scripts keep their fonts. Unset, nothing changes.
