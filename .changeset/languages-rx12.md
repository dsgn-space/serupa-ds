Added: language packs for Bengali (`bn`), Thai (`th`), Persian (`fa`), Hebrew (`he`), Czech (`cs`), Romanian (`ro`), Greek (`el`), Hungarian (`hu`), Danish (`da`), Finnish (`fi`) and Norwegian Bokmål (`nb`), each at `@diki-stwn/serupa-ds/labels/<language>`. Persian and Hebrew read right to left. These 11 are beta: machine-checked and compared with established UI terms, but not yet read by a native speaker, so their words may change in a minor release.

Fixed: in every pack, the table's "Select all" and "All selected" now show the count as the table formats it. German puts a space before `…` throughout, and a few words now match the term used elsewhere in their pack (Dutch, Polish, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Portuguese, French).
