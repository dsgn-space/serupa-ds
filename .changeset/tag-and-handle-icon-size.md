TagGroup, CommandPalette and Kanban: icons that were drawn at an icon's 24px default, because no size reached them, get their size. A tag's own icon, a selected tag's check and its × are 16px beside the 14px label (they filled the whole 24px tag), set by the new `--tag-group-icon-size`. A command's icon is 20px in its 44px row, as a Sidebar item's, set by the new `--command-palette-icon-size`. Kanban's drag handle is 20px, as SortableList's. The Pricing block's feature checks are 16px beside their 14px lines.
