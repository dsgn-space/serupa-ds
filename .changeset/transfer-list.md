Added: TransferList, two lists (Available and Chosen) with Add and Remove between them, for picking a set out of many: table columns, permissions, a team's members. Each list is a group of checkboxes with an All box, named with its count. Each move is said aloud, and Add and Remove keep focus when there's nothing to move. With `name`, a form submits the chosen values in order. Its words come in every language pack.
