Four new patterns. Appointment: a time picked by day and then by time, with days that have no free time closed, taken times listed and named taken, your details checked next to each field, and a confirmation that takes focus. Profile: a photo, name, bio, location and link edited in one form beside a preview, with limits said and counted, problems said on save, where the save has got to in a status, and Discard changes once something changed. Feedback survey: a rating as named star radios, choices as named groups, the email asked for only once a reply is allowed, and thanks that take focus. Invoice: due, paid or overdue as a word and a sentence, the lines as a table, the totals ending with the amount due, a named download, and Pay that marks it paid only once it is.
