States: a picked-up Kanban card now looks like a picked-up SortableList item (a `primary` border and the `elevation-raised` shadow, with a Highlight outline in forced colors), and the States table lists Dragged. New `--kanban-card-border-active` token; `--kanban-card-shadow` now defaults to `elevation-raised`. New `state-layer` class and `opacity-state-hover`, `opacity-state-pressed` and `opacity-state-dragged` tokens give hover, pressed and dragged states to surfaces in colors the app picks itself.
