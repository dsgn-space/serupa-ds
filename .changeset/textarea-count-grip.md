Textarea: the count sits under the box, at its end, in `text-muted` like a Field hint (was inside the box, in `placeholder`). In a Field it shares a row with the hint or the error: the message at the start, the count at the end (mirrored right to left). With `actions` it still starts the actions row inside the box. The resize handle is the browser's own again, at its default size. New tokens `--textarea-count-gap` and `--field-end-gap`.
