- Added: SignaturePad (beta), a signature drawn with a mouse, pen or finger or typed as a full name (the keyboard way, WCAG 2.5.7), with Undo and Clear, giving `{ method, svg, name }` to `onValueChange` and the SVG to a form; and `signatureToPng`, which makes a PNG of the SVG. Its words are in all 20 languages, with 10 component tokens.
