Three more opt-in additions from the Material Design 3 study. `List` takes `divided="inset"`: each line starts where the text starts, after the avatar or icon. `Carousel` takes `peek`: 48px of the next slide shows at the end so people can tell there is more to swipe to, and that slide stays out of the tab order. `Slider` takes `origin`: a single thumb's fill runs from that value to the thumb, for scales with a middle, such as -10 to +10.
