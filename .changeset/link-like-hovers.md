Two controls that gave no sign when pointed at now answer as their kin do. TagGroup's "Show more" is underlined as a link is (1px, 2px below the words) and on hover turns `--primary-hover` with a 2px underline (`--tag-group-more-hover`). Kanban's drag handle fills with `--secondary` on hover and `--secondary-hover` while held, as SortableList's does (`--kanban-handle-bg-hover`, `--kanban-handle-bg-pressed`).
