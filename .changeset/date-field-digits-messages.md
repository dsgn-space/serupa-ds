DateField: the boxes take digits only, up to 2, 2 and 4 (letters typed or pasted are dropped). Once focus leaves the date, or its form is sent, it says what's wrong under the boxes and marks the box to fix ("Day must be between 1 and 31", "Enter a real date: February 2025 has 28 days"), in all 20 languages, and a form isn't sent while the date is wrong. A Field's own error takes its place. New `monthInput="select"` shows the month as a menu of the locale's month names.
