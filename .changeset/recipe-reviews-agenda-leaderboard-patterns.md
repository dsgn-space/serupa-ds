Four new patterns to copy: Recipe, Reviews, Conference agenda and Leaderboard. A recipe rescales every amount when you change the servings and says so, and makes each ingredient and step a checkbox named by its text. Reviews show the average as stars named in words and the split as meters with their values in words, and make Helpful a toggle named by the review it's for. A conference agenda puts each day in a tab and each start time in a heading, saves sessions with a toggle named by the session, and says on both sessions when two you saved clash. A leaderboard is a table with names as row headers, says ties as "Joint", says moves in words, and shows your row after the top places.
