Two new patterns on RangeCalendar. Booking: a stay picked on two months side by side, with the nights already booked struck through and listed in words (a stay can still end on the day a booked one starts), dates that take one in or are too short said at once, the price worked out as a description list, and a confirmation that takes focus once booked. Leave request: time off with the working days counted, weekends and holidays closed and left out of the count, the holidays in view and inside the leave named, the balance and what's waiting in words, annual leave past what's left said with a way out, and a sent request listed as waiting with focus on it.
