Calendar and RangeCalendar: `headingLevel`, 2 (the default) to 6, sets the month title's heading level, so a calendar inline under an `h2` can title its month `h3`. EventCalendar, Card and EmptyState already take it.

Chat's stories draw their frame in the border color, so it isn't black in light mode and white in dark. Their day dividers format their own dates, so they read the same way as the message times.
