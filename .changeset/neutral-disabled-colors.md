- **Breaking:** disabled controls are drawn in neutral colors instead of being faded to 50%. A disabled field, checkbox, radio, switch, slider, tab, menu item, list item, pagination arrow, calendar day and the rest (29 components, as Button already was) now keep full opacity and use three new color roles: `disabled-text` for the label and marks, `disabled-surface` for a fill, and `disabled-border` for an edge. `disabled-text` reads at least 3:1 on every surface in every theme, and more with `prefers-contrast: more`, so a disabled label stays readable, where a 50% fade could drop it under 2:1 on a brand color. Each component reads them through its own tokens (`--checkbox-text-disabled`, `--input-border-disabled`, `--switch-fill-disabled` and so on), listed in its README. `opacity-disabled` stays for your own CSS; ColorPicker and SignaturePad keep it, since their content is the user's own colors. Pagination's `--pagination-disabled` (an opacity) is gone: set `--pagination-text-disabled` (a color) instead.
  **Migration:** nothing to change unless you styled a disabled control. If your CSS set `opacity` on a Serupa control's disabled state to undo or match the fade, remove it. If you set `--pagination-disabled`, set `--pagination-text-disabled` to a color. To bring back the old look on one component, set its tokens to the enabled colors and add `opacity: var(--opacity-disabled)` to its disabled selector in your CSS. A screenshot test of a disabled control will change.
