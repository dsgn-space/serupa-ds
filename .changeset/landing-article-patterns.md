Two new patterns for the pages outside the app, which `npx serupa-add` writes into a project: **Landing** (a hero with one call to action in the same words everywhere, the features, the Pricing and FAQ blocks, and the Footer) and **Article** (a post with a breadcrumb, a dated byline in the page's language, "On this page" and "Read next").
