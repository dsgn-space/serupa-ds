Five new patterns to copy: Support ticket, Product compare, Store locator, Expense report and Email preferences. A support ticket says where it's got to in words, shows the conversation as articles named by who wrote them, and moves focus to what replaces Mark as solved and Reopen. Product compare is a table with products heading the columns and features heading the rows, says yes and no in words, and can show only what differs. The store locator is a search and a list rather than a map alone, says open or closed in words with when that changes, and sorts by your location. An expense report names each problem next to its field, totals the expenses in the table's footer, and checks the report before sending it. Email preferences says which address it's about, gives each kind of email a switch with how often it comes, and stops them all with one button that can be undone.
