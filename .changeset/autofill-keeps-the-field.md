Input, Textarea, Select and PinInput: a field the browser fills in (a saved login, an address, a code from a text message) keeps its look. The browser's pale blue or yellow fill covered only the native field, a rectangle inside the box that stopped short of its padding, icons and corners, and in PinInput it lay over the boxes with the code drawn twice. Everything built on Input's field (PasswordInput, PhoneInput, NumberInput, Combobox, the date and time pickers) gets the same.
