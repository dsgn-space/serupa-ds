Added: TruncatedText (beta), for a file name, an email address or a title that may not fit its space. It cuts the text with an ellipsis (one line, or up to six with `lines`) and shows the whole text beside it on hover, keyboard focus or a tap, only while it is cut: cut text takes a tab stop, and text that fits stays plain text. Inside a link or button it adds no tab stop, and that control's keyboard focus shows the whole text. Screen readers read the text once, from the element.
