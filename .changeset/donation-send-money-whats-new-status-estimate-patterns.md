Five new patterns to copy: Donation, Send money, What's new, Status page and Price estimate. A donation describes each amount by what it pays for, works out the card fee so the charity gets the whole gift, and names the total on Give. Send money says your balance first, shows what they'll get as you type, and moves nothing until a review step. What's new puts each change in an article under its month, says its kind in words, and marks what's new since your last visit. A status page leads with the state in words, gives each service a 90-day strip with every day named, and lists each incident's updates in order. A price estimate shows the cost as a table that follows each choice and says the total with how it's billed.
