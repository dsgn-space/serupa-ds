Added: Watermark repeats the viewer's name, or a word such as "Draft", faintly across a document preview or the whole window (`fullPage`), so a screenshot or a printout shows where it came from. It is decorative: screen readers, find in page, selection, copy and clicks all pass through it. It follows the theme, renders on the server, prints, and steps aside under forced colors. Beta.
