Four new patterns to copy: Download your data, Approvals, Transactions and Roles and permissions. Download your data lets people choose what to include with each part's size, says the total as the choice changes, and gives each export's state in words with the date its link runs out. Approvals puts each request in an article named by who asked for what, approves with Undo, declines with a reason, and moves focus to the next request. Transactions leads with the month's totals, keeps money in and money out in their own columns, and says how many show after a search. Roles and permissions is a table of checkboxes read with their role and permission, where a permission brings what it needs and says so, and nothing changes until Save.
