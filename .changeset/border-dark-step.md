"serupa-ds": minor
---

`border` is a step quieter in dark: `zinc-800` instead of `zinc-700` in blue and violet, and white at 8% instead of 12% in emerald. Cards, tables, dividers and bars keep a quiet edge a step off their `zinc-900` fill, instead of a line brighter than the controls inside them. Light themes don't change. A linked card still steps up to `border-strong` on hover, and nothing that marks a control reads `border`: fields use `field-border`, other controls `border-control`.
