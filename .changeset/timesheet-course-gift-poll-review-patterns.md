Five new patterns to copy: Timesheet, Course overview, Gift card, Poll and Write a review. A timesheet is a table whose hours fields are named by their project and day, totals each row, day and the week as you type, and links a summary problem to the day that's over 24 hours. A course overview opens the module with the next lesson, shows progress as a bar with its value in words, and names the lesson Continue opens. A gift card shows a preview that follows the form and brings a number field for another amount. A poll shows its results as meters with votes and shares in words, marks yours, and lets you change it. Write a review names what's reviewed first, makes the stars a group of radios, and lands the summary's link on a star.
