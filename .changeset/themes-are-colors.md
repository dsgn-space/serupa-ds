- **Breaking.** A theme is its colors and nothing else. Violet and Emerald now differ from Blue only in their brand colors, in light and dark: every corner, shadow, border, surface, space and motion is Blue's. Blue stays the default. An app on `violet` or `emerald` looks different after upgrading:
  - **Violet** is flat. The tactile look is gone: no lift and sheen on buttons, no sunk fields and tracks, no raised thumbs, no hairline panes, no card lift, no 1px press, no spring on the Switch thumb and no drawn Checkbox tick.
  - **Emerald** (it was called Modern) takes Blue's corners (6px controls and 8px surfaces, were 8 and 12px), spacing (`space-surface` 24px and `space-section` 32px, were 32 and 48px), elevation (each role one shadow step stronger again), motion durations and easings, solid `zinc` borders (were see-through ink) and dark surfaces (`zinc-900` cards on the `zinc-950` page, `zinc-800` raised and overlay surfaces). Its teal accent, its darker light-mode primary (`teal-700`) and its teal-led chart colors stay. Its themes are named Emerald Light and Emerald Dark; the ids (`emerald-light`, `emerald-dark`, `data-brand="emerald"`) don't change.
- Removed: the `data-depth` attribute (`tactile`, `flat`). Every brand is flat.
- Removed: the 12 `depth-*` tokens: `depth-control`, `depth-control-hover`, `depth-control-pressed`, `depth-sheen`, `depth-sheen-pressed`, `depth-part`, `depth-field`, `depth-well`, `depth-surface`, `depth-card`, `depth-card-lift` and `depth-press`. Outside the tactile look each was empty (no shadow, no image, 0), so nothing takes their place.
- Removed: the 46 component tokens that set a component's tactile layer, also empty outside the tactile look: `--button-depth`, `--button-depth-hover`, `--button-depth-pressed`, `--calendar-depth-selected`, `--card-depth`, `--card-depth-hover`, `--cascade-select-panel-depth`, `--chart-tip-depth`, `--checkbox-depth`, `--checkbox-depth-checked`, `--combobox-listbox-depth`, `--date-picker-popup-depth`, `--dialog-depth`, `--drawer-depth`, `--hover-card-depth`, `--input-depth`, `--kanban-card-depth`, `--kbd-depth`, `--menu-depth`, `--meter-track-depth`, `--navbar-menu-depth`, `--offline-banner-depth`, `--pagination-depth-current`, `--period-picker-depth`, `--period-picker-depth-selected`, `--period-picker-panel-depth`, `--pin-input-depth`, `--popover-depth`, `--progress-track-depth`, `--radio-depth`, `--radio-depth-checked`, `--segmented-control-indicator-depth`, `--select-depth`, `--select-picker-depth`, `--slider-thumb-depth`, `--slider-track-depth`, `--sortable-list-item-depth`, `--stepper-depth-active`, `--switch-thumb-depth`, `--switch-track-depth`, `--textarea-depth`, `--time-picker-list-depth`, `--toast-depth`, `--toggle-button-depth-pressed`, `--tooltip-depth`, `--tour-depth`.
- Removed: `motion-toggle` (use `motion-press`), `ease-toggle` (use `ease-move`) and `motion-draw` (it was `duration-0`), with Checkbox's `--checkbox-draw-duration` and its tick-drawing animation. They only differed in the tactile look. The `ease-toggle` utility class goes with its token (use `ease-move`).
- Removed (serupa-brand): `--look`. A theme made from a brand color always takes Blue's neutrals, corners, shadows and motion; `--corners` still sets its corners.
- Changed: `data-radius="default"` in a section puts the default corners back (6, 8 and 12px) in every theme.

**Migration:** run `npx serupa-migrate src --write`. It rewrites `var(--motion-toggle)`, `var(--ease-toggle)` and `var(--motion-draw)` to the tokens above and the `ease-toggle` class to `ease-move`, and lists each place that sets or reads a removed depth token or sets `data-depth`: delete those declarations and attributes. Nothing else changes in your code. To keep Emerald's rounder corners, set `data-radius="round"` on the page (12px controls, 16px surfaces). A theme made with `serupa-brand --look modern` should be made again without `--look`.
