- **Breaking.** A theme is a brand color and a mode, and nothing else. Every theme now differs from Blue only in its brand colors (`primary`, `primary-hover`, `primary-subtle`, `on-primary`, `field-action`, `focus-ring`, `link-visited`), in light and dark: every corner, shadow, border, surface, space, chart color and motion is Blue's. Blue stays the default. An app on `violet` or `emerald` looks different after upgrading:
  - **Violet** is flat. The tactile look is gone: no lift and sheen on buttons, no sunk fields and tracks, no raised thumbs, no hairline panes, no card lift, no 1px press, no spring on the Switch thumb and no drawn Checkbox tick.
  - **Emerald** (it was called Modern) takes Blue's corners (6px controls and 8px surfaces, were 8 and 12px), spacing (`space-surface` 24px and `space-section` 32px, were 32 and 48px), elevation (each role one shadow step stronger again), motion durations and easings, solid `zinc` borders (were see-through ink), dark surfaces (`zinc-900` cards on the `zinc-950` page, `zinc-800` raised and overlay surfaces) and chart colors. Its themes are named Emerald Light and Emerald Dark.
  - **Emerald draws in the emerald ramp**, a greener green (`emerald-700` in light, `emerald-400` in dark). It drew in `teal` before; that color is the new `teal` brand.
- New: a theme for every accent color of the palette, each light and dark. Besides `blue`, `violet` and `emerald` there are `red`, `orange`, `amber`, `yellow`, `lime`, `green`, `teal`, `cyan`, `sky`, `indigo`, `purple`, `fuchsia`, `pink` and `rose`: `data-brand="rose"`, `data-theme="rose-dark"`, `Serupa.theme.set({ brand: 'rose' })`, `serupa-create my-app --brand rose`. Each meets WCAG 2.2 AA on every pair the three first brands are held to, and steps up within its own ramp with `data-contrast="more"`. In light themes the lighter ramps (`orange`, `amber`, `yellow`, `lime`, `green`, `emerald`, `teal`, `cyan`, `sky`, `pink`) use step 700 for `primary`, so white text on it and brand-colored text on the page both reach 4.5:1; the others use 600.
- Changed: `THEMES` and `forEachTheme()` of `@diki-stwn/serupa-ds/testing` cover all 34 themes. Pass `themes` to draw only the ones your app uses.
- Changed: `tokens.css` is about 1.2 KiB larger compressed (13.5 KiB) and `theme.js` 0.1 KiB, for the 28 new themes.
- Removed: the `data-depth` attribute (`tactile`, `flat`). Every brand is flat.
- Removed: the 12 `depth-*` tokens: `depth-control`, `depth-control-hover`, `depth-control-pressed`, `depth-sheen`, `depth-sheen-pressed`, `depth-part`, `depth-field`, `depth-well`, `depth-surface`, `depth-card`, `depth-card-lift` and `depth-press`. Outside the tactile look each was empty (no shadow, no image, 0), so nothing takes their place.
- Removed: the 46 component tokens that set a component's tactile layer, also empty outside the tactile look: `--button-depth`, `--button-depth-hover`, `--button-depth-pressed`, `--calendar-depth-selected`, `--card-depth`, `--card-depth-hover`, `--cascade-select-panel-depth`, `--chart-tip-depth`, `--checkbox-depth`, `--checkbox-depth-checked`, `--combobox-listbox-depth`, `--date-picker-popup-depth`, `--dialog-depth`, `--drawer-depth`, `--hover-card-depth`, `--input-depth`, `--kanban-card-depth`, `--kbd-depth`, `--menu-depth`, `--meter-track-depth`, `--navbar-menu-depth`, `--offline-banner-depth`, `--pagination-depth-current`, `--period-picker-depth`, `--period-picker-depth-selected`, `--period-picker-panel-depth`, `--pin-input-depth`, `--popover-depth`, `--progress-track-depth`, `--radio-depth`, `--radio-depth-checked`, `--segmented-control-indicator-depth`, `--select-depth`, `--select-picker-depth`, `--slider-thumb-depth`, `--slider-track-depth`, `--sortable-list-item-depth`, `--stepper-depth-active`, `--switch-thumb-depth`, `--switch-track-depth`, `--textarea-depth`, `--time-picker-list-depth`, `--toast-depth`, `--toggle-button-depth-pressed`, `--tooltip-depth`, `--tour-depth`.
- Removed: `motion-toggle` (use `motion-press`), `ease-toggle` (use `ease-move`) and `motion-draw` (it was `duration-0`), with Checkbox's `--checkbox-draw-duration` and its tick-drawing animation. They only differed in the tactile look. The `ease-toggle` utility class goes with its token (use `ease-move`).
- Removed (serupa-brand): `--look`. A theme made from a brand color always takes Blue's neutrals, corners, shadows and motion; `--corners` still sets its corners.
- Changed: `data-radius="default"` in a section puts the default corners back (6, 8 and 12px) in every theme.

**Migration:** run `npx serupa-migrate src --write`. It rewrites `var(--motion-toggle)`, `var(--ease-toggle)` and `var(--motion-draw)` to the tokens above and the `ease-toggle` class to `ease-move`, and lists each place that sets or reads a removed depth token or sets `data-depth`: delete those declarations and attributes. Nothing else changes in your code. It also lists each place that names the `emerald` brand (`data-brand="emerald"`, `emerald-light`, `emerald-dark`): write `teal` there to keep the color it had, or leave it for the greener emerald. To keep Emerald's rounder corners, set `data-radius="round"` on the page (12px controls, 16px surfaces). A theme made with `serupa-brand --look modern` should be made again without `--look`.
