Two new patterns for a shop. Product page: a product's pictures with their own alt text, a sale price with "Was" in words, each choice (color, size) a named group of radios where a sold-out one says so, a missing choice asked for next to its group, and Add to cart that says what went in and updates the cart's count. Order status: the order's stage as a sentence above its steps, a delay with its reason, what happened as a dated list, the items and totals, and a cancel that asks first, is shown only once it's done, and says why it's gone once the order has shipped.
