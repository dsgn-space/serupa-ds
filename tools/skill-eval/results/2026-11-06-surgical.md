## What changed

Every answer, with the skill or without, before rule 8 or after, kept to the change: it rewrote 1 to 3 old lines (an import, a type, the list's map), never the legacy header, and never reformatted the file. So rule 8 states what agents already do here, and `surgical` stands guard against a change that starts migrating or reformatting. Without the skill, legacy-page used a plain `<input>` in all 10 answers; with the skill, SearchInput in all 10.

These tasks are small and the file is short. A larger file, or an agent with tools that edits in place, may behave differently; PN2's agentic mode is where to measure that.
