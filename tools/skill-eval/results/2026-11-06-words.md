## What changed

The words check passes 10 of 10 with the skill, before rule 7 and after it. The skill's error rule and the model's own habits already avoid the tells the check looks for. Without the skill, one answer failed it the first time ("Thanks! Your message has been sent."). The rule shows in the wording the check lets through: before it, 4 of 5 load-error answers said "Something went wrong while loading your projects"; with it, all 5 say the projects couldn't load, as Content and voice asks. Rule 7 adds about 100 words to the skill, which stays under its 3,500-word budget.
