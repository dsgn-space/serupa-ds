# RFC: 18.0.0

**Status:** Open for comments

A dark theme's brand color becomes the light theme's own, so the two modes read as one brand, and every breaking change held for a major goes out with it (Diki, 22 November 2026). Every breaking changeset until the release adds a section here.

## Dark themes fill with the light theme's brand shade, and brand text is `primary-text` (`.changeset/dark-fills-match-light.md`)

- **What breaks:** in dark themes `primary`, `primary-hover` and `primary-pressed` are the light theme's shades, a little lighter, under a white `on-primary`; they were brand 400, 300 and 200 under brand-950 text. Yellow Dark keeps those, and Yellow Light fills with `yellow-500` under `yellow-950` text inside the new `primary-edge` (`yellow-700`, transparent in every other theme). CSS that sets text from `--primary` (or uses `text-primary`) gets the fill's shade, about 3.6:1 on a dark card. A theme made by `serupa-brand` before 18.0.0, or written by hand, sets `--primary` and not the new `--primary-text`, so its links and outline buttons draw in blue until it's made again.
- **Why:** a dark theme's brand color was two or three palette steps lighter than its light theme's, with the text on it flipped from white to dark, so a primary button looked like another brand's after dark. One token did two jobs with different needs: a fill has to carry white text, and text on a dark page has to be light. Danger already worked this way (`red-600` under white in both modes, `danger-text` for text). [ADR-0026](../adr/0026-dark-fills-are-the-light-themes.md) has the rule and the numbers.
- **Migration:** `serupa-migrate --from 17` turns `color: var(--primary)` into `color: var(--primary-text)` and the `text-primary` classes into `text-primary-text`, and lists each theme file that sets `--primary` without `--primary-text`. Make a `serupa-brand` theme again with the same command; in a hand-written theme set `--primary-text` and `--primary-text-hover` to what `--primary` and `--primary-hover` were, and in dark give `--primary` the light theme's shade with `--on-primary: var(--white)` (`serupa-migrate` makes it)

## The names deprecated for 18.0.0 are removed (`.changeset/names-deprecated-for-18.md`)

- **What breaks:** `onMedia` (Button, IconButton, Link, Divider and what is built on Button), `empty` (TagGroup, NotificationCenter), Toaster's `placement="top-center"` and `"bottom-center"`, and the `ToasterPosition` and `FabPosition` types are gone.
- **Why:** one idea has one name in every component. They were deprecated a few days before; Diki asked for them to go in 18.0.0 with the other breaking changes, so the four-week wait is suspended through 18.0.0 (`tools/release-policy.json`).
- **Migration:** `npx serupa-migrate --from 17` makes every rename: `surface`, `emptyText`, `top` and `bottom`, `ToasterPlacement` and `FabPlacement`.
