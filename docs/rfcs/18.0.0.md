# RFC: 18.0.0

**Status:** Open for comments

A dark theme's brand color becomes the light theme's own, so the two modes read as one brand, and every breaking change held for a major goes out with it (Diki, 22 November 2026). Every breaking changeset until the release adds a section here.

## Dark themes fill with the light theme's brand shade, and brand text is `primary-text` (`.changeset/dark-fills-match-light.md`)

- **What breaks:** in dark themes `primary`, `primary-hover` and `primary-pressed` are the light theme's shades, a little lighter, under a white `on-primary`; they were brand 400, 300 and 200 under brand-950 text. Yellow Dark keeps those, and Yellow Light fills with `yellow-500` under `yellow-950` text inside the new `primary-edge` (`yellow-700`, transparent in every other theme). CSS that sets text from `--primary` (or uses `text-primary`) gets the fill's shade, about 3.6:1 on a dark card. A theme made by `serupa-brand` before 18.0.0, or written by hand, sets `--primary` and not the new `--primary-text`, so its links and outline buttons draw in blue until it's made again.
- **Why:** a dark theme's brand color was two or three palette steps lighter than its light theme's, with the text on it flipped from white to dark, so a primary button looked like another brand's after dark. One token did two jobs with different needs: a fill has to carry white text, and text on a dark page has to be light. Danger already worked this way (`red-600` under white in both modes, `danger-text` for text). [ADR-0026](../adr/0026-dark-fills-are-the-light-themes.md) has the rule and the numbers.
- **Migration:** `serupa-migrate --from 17` turns `color: var(--primary)` into `color: var(--primary-text)` and the `text-primary` classes into `text-primary-text`, and lists each theme file that sets `--primary` without `--primary-text`. Make a `serupa-brand` theme again with the same command; in a hand-written theme set `--primary-text` and `--primary-text-hover` to what `--primary` and `--primary-hover` were, and in dark give `--primary` the light theme's shade with `--on-primary: var(--white)` (`serupa-migrate` makes it)

## Dark themes fill success, warning, info and Badge's palette solids with the light theme's shade (`.changeset/status-fills-match-light.md`)

- **What breaks:** in dark themes `success` and `warning` are a step darker (`green-600`, `amber-600`, the light theme's), and `info` is the light theme's `sky-700` lightened to 3:1 on a raised card (#1e77b0) under a white `on-info` (it was `sky-400` under `sky-950`). Badge's palette solids are 600 or 700 under white in dark too. Text set from `--info` on a dark page is 3.6:1; dark text on `--info` is 2.9:1.
- **Why:** the status fills were lighter in dark than in light, and info's text flipped, so a status read as another color after dark. They follow the rule the brand fill takes in 18.0.0 ([ADR-0026](../adr/0026-dark-fills-are-the-light-themes.md)).
- **Migration:** status text uses the `*-text` tokens and text on a fill the `on-*` tokens (by hand). A theme that wants the old dark shades sets `--success`, `--warning`, `--info` and `--on-info` itself.

## The names deprecated for 18.0.0 are removed (`.changeset/names-deprecated-for-18.md`)

- **What breaks:** `onMedia` (Button, IconButton, Link, Divider and what is built on Button), `empty` (TagGroup, NotificationCenter), Toaster's `placement="top-center"` and `"bottom-center"`, and the `ToasterPosition` and `FabPosition` types are gone.
- **Why:** one idea has one name in every component. They were deprecated a few days before; Diki asked for them to go in 18.0.0 with the other breaking changes, so the four-week wait is suspended through 18.0.0 (`tools/release-policy.json`).
- **Migration:** `npx serupa-migrate --from 17` makes every rename: `surface`, `emptyText`, `top` and `bottom`, `ToasterPlacement` and `FabPlacement`.

## Danger interrupts, everything else waits (`.changeset/danger-interrupts.md`)

- **What breaks:** Alert with `tone="warning"` is `role="status"`, not `role="alert"`, so a screen reader says it at the next pause. SaveStatus says its error assertively.
- **Why:** a warning interrupted in Alert and waited in Toast, and a failed save was said more quietly than a toast about the same failure. One rule decides: only danger interrupts.
- **Migration:** nothing to rename. A warning that must stop people passes `role="alert"` itself (by hand).

## Tokens nothing uses are removed (`.changeset/tokens-cleanup.md`)

- **What breaks:** `--field-action` (the same color as `--primary-text` in all 34 themes) and its classes; the `gray`, `neutral` and `stone` ramps (44 tokens nothing reads); `--rounded` and the bare `rounded` class; `--motion-ripple` and `--motion-ripple-fade`. `serupa-brand` drops `--corners` and `--motion`, and theme.js stops reading the 4.x `diki-theme` key.
- **Why:** each is unread outside its own definition, or a second name for one that stays, and a brand sets its colors and nothing else.
- **Migration:** `serupa-migrate` (`tokens-removed`) rewrites `field-action` to `primary-text`, `rounded` to `rounded-sm` and the ripple tokens to duration steps, and lists each `gray`, `neutral` or `stone` token to move to `zinc` or `slate`.

## design-tokens/ keeps a theme as its mode and its brand's overlay (`.changeset/design-tokens-overlays.md`)

- **What breaks:** the 34 `design-tokens/themes/<theme>.tokens.json` files go. `themes/light` and `themes/dark` hold the alias colors, and `brands/<theme>` the ones a brand sets apart.
- **Why:** 34 files of 101 tokens differed in 7 to 9 values each: 55% of the folder. It goes from 2.4 MB to 1.3 MB.
- **Migration:** a tool that loaded one theme file loads `serupa.tokens.json`, the mode file, then the brand overlay (later files win), as `tools/tokens/style-dictionary.config.mjs` shows (by hand).

## The package carries Inter's subsets only (`.changeset/whole-inter-fonts.md`)

- **What breaks:** `fonts/InterVariable.woff2` and `fonts/InterVariable-Italic.woff2`, the whole fonts, leave the package. The subsets by script, which the CSS loads, stay.
- **Why:** nothing in the package links the whole fonts. Together they are 740 KB of every install.
- **Migration:** a page that linked a whole font links the subsets the CSS already names, or takes Inter from its own source (by hand).
