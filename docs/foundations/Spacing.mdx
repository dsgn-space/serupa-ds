## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Space with a step of the scale: `gap-2`, `p-4`, `<Stack gap={3}>`. | Write a number that's not on it (`margin-top: 13px`). |
| Put the space between things with `gap` on their parent. | Give each child its own margin, which doubles up and leaks out at the edges. |
| Keep related things closer together than unrelated ones. | Space everything the same, so groups disappear. |
