## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Raise with the level for the layer: `elevation-raised` for a card that floats, `elevation-overlay` for menus, `elevation-modal` for dialogs. | Write your own `box-shadow`. It won't match the others, and `data-shadow` or forced colors can't adjust it. |
| Let higher things cast bigger shadows, so the order on screen reads at a glance. | Put a big shadow on something flat on the page. |
| Separate flat panels with a border or `surface-muted`. | Use a shadow just to make a box stand out. |
