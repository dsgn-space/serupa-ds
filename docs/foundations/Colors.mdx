## Chart colors

Charts draw with their own tokens, so a chart looks the same next to the components in every theme: `chart-1` to `chart-8` for the series, in that order, `chart-seq-1` to `chart-seq-5` for an amount from low to high (a heat map), and `chart-div-1` to `chart-div-7` for change either side of a middle (`chart-div-4`). `chart-grid`, `chart-axis`, `chart-label` and `chart-gap` are the rest of the chart. They make no utility classes: read them as variables, `fill: var(--chart-1)`.

- **Each series is 3:1 on the page and on a raised card** in all six themes (WCAG 1.4.11), and so are the ramps' strong ends. Labels are 4.5:1.
- **Neighbours differ by hue for every reader.** Two series next to each other are at least 15 apart in OKLab (×100) as seen, and 8 apart for people with protanopia or deuteranopia. The first three are that far apart as every pair, so a scatter chart of up to three series works by color too; past three, name the series on the chart or split it into small charts.
- **In light themes neighbours also alternate darker and lighter,** at least 1.7:1 apart. Dark themes use mid-tones instead (OKLCH lightness 0.48 to 0.67), because the pale steps of blue and violet look the same to many color-blind readers.
- **Touching marks are kept apart.** Eight different hues can't all be 3:1 from each other on one page, so bars, slices and areas that touch are kept apart by a 2px `chart-gap` line, the page color, which each of them is 3:1 against. On a card, set `--chart-gap` to the card's color.
- **Color is never the only clue.** Name each series next to its line or in a legend, and give lines markers or dashes, so two series can be told apart without telling the colors apart.
- **Forced colors** map the series to the user's own colors (five of them differ), the grid to `GrayText` and the gap to `Canvas`. **Print** keeps the theme on the page, as the rest of the page does: a light theme prints its light colors, and a dark theme (`data-theme="*-dark"`, which `data-mode="dark"` sets) prints as it shows, with its dark background kept, so every series is still 3:1 on it.
