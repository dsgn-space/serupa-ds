# Messages and feedback

Pick a message by two things: how soon people need to act, and how long the message stays true. A message that is too loud for what it says trains people to ignore the loud ones; one that is too quiet gets missed.

## Pick by urgency and how long it lasts

| The message is… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| About one field's answer | The [Field](?path=/docs/components-forms-field--docs)'s `error`, and on submit an [ErrorSummary](?path=/docs/components-forms-errorsummary--docs) |
| A confirmation of what people just did, when the page doesn't show it: "Link copied", "Invite sent" | A [Toast](?path=/docs/components-feedback-toast--docs) |
| About the page or a task, staying until it's dealt with: a failed payment, an expiring trial | An [Alert](?path=/docs/components-feedback-alert--docs) near what it's about |
| A decision people must make before they can go on | A [Dialog](?path=/docs/components-overlays-dialog--docs) with `alert` |
| Where saving is, on a page that saves by itself | [SaveStatus](?path=/docs/components-feedback-savestatus--docs) |
| The connection is gone, for the whole app | [OfflineBanner](?path=/docs/components-feedback-offlinebanner--docs) |
| Something people may want later, after its toast is gone | [NotificationCenter](?path=/docs/components-overlays-notificationcenter--docs) |
| A short status in a table or a card: Paid, Overdue | A [Badge](?path=/docs/components-feedback-badge--docs) |
| A list or a search with nothing in it | An [EmptyState](?path=/docs/components-feedback-emptystate--docs) |

## Tone

Use the tone that matches what happened, and no louder: `success` for something done, `warning` for something to watch, `danger` for something that failed or will be lost, and `info` or neutral for the rest. Color is never the only sign: the info, success, warning and danger tones each have their own icon, and the words carry the meaning.

## Writing the message

- Say what happened, then what to do: "Payment failed. Update your card to keep your plan."
- Keep a toast to a few words: "Changes saved", not "Your changes have been saved successfully."
- Name the action on its button ("Update card", "Try again"), not "OK".
- An error never blames the person, and never shows a code alone. Put a code after the words, for support, if it helps.

## How long it stays

- A toast goes by itself after 5 seconds, so it never holds anything people must read. One that needs action, such as a failed upload with Retry, takes `autoDismiss: false` and stays until it's closed.
- An Alert stays until what it says is no longer true, or until people dismiss it, if it's dismissible.
- An error on a field stays until the answer is right.

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| A Toast for "Link copied" | An Alert that stays on the page for a confirmation |
| An Alert by the billing section for a failed payment | A Toast that's gone before people read it |
| "Couldn't send. Check your connection and try again." | "Error 500" |
| `danger` for something that failed | `danger` for anything you want people to notice |
