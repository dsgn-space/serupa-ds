# AI features

An assistant, a generated summary or a suggested reply is still part of your app: people should know when it's at work, stay in charge of what it does, and be able to undo or improve what it made. The [Chat](?path=/docs/components-content-chat--docs) parts cover a conversation; the rules below go for any feature that generates.

## Say where it's at work

- Name what made a result where it shows ("Summary written by the assistant"), in words, not only by an icon or a color.
- Say in a sentence what it can't do or may get wrong, where people decide to use it, not in a policy page.
- Show confidence in plain words ("Likely", "Not sure") when it matters, never as a bare number.

## Keep people in charge

- Ask before it acts on anything that leaves the page or costs something: a payment, a message sent, a file deleted. [ChatApproval](?path=/docs/components-content-chat--docs) puts what it will do in words, with Approve and Deny.
- Let people stop it while it works (ChatComposer's `onStop`), and keep what was typed.
- Show what it did on the way, step by step, with `steps` on a reply, so a wrong turn can be seen.

## Let people change results

- Offer another version rather than one fixed answer: Regenerate in [ChatReplyActions](?path=/docs/components-content-chat--docs) asks again, and `branch` keeps the earlier versions to go back to.
- Let people edit a result, or correct the assistant, and correct that correction again. Never lock a generated value.
- Undo an action it took the same way as any other: [toast.undo](?path=/docs/guides-destructive-actions--docs) and Ctrl+Z.

## Ask for feedback, lightly

- Helpful and Not helpful under a reply (`ChatReplyActions` `onFeedback`), always optional, one press each, and pressing again takes it back.
- Act on it at once: after "Not helpful", offer Regenerate or a way to say more, rather than a form first.
- Say it was heard ("Thanks, noted", which ChatReplyActions says) and don't ask again for the same reply.

## While it works

- Say what it's doing, specifically: "Reading 3 files", not only "Thinking…". The reply's `thinking`, `reasoning` and `steps` carry this.
- Stream long answers as they come (`streaming` on the reply); screen readers wait for the whole answer.
- For a wait with no visible text, follow [Loading and waiting](?path=/docs/guides-loading-and-waiting--docs).
