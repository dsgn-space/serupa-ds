# Footer

The site's footer: link groups, a language picker, a theme picker and the legal line. It's built from Heading, Link, Field, Select and Button.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- The end of every page of a site: links people look for last, the language and theme pickers, and the legal line.
- Not for an app's main navigation: that belongs in the Navbar or a Sidebar, where people look first.

## What each part is for

- **A `<footer>`**, which is the page's contentinfo landmark when it sits outside `main`, so screen reader users can jump to it.
- **Each link group is a navigation landmark named by its heading** (1.3.1). In a list of landmarks, "Product navigation" and "Company navigation" are told apart, rather than four that are each just "navigation". Pick `headingLevel` to fit the page: the default is 2.
- **Each language is written in itself and marked with its `lang`** (3.1.2), so "Bahasa Indonesia" is said the Indonesian way and "日本語" the Japanese way.
- **A new language waits for Change** (3.2.2). A new language usually means a new page, so it happens only when the person presses the button, never as they move through the list.
- **A new theme applies at once.** It only changes how the page looks, not where you are. "Match my device" follows the device's light or dark setting.
- **Visible labels on both pickers** (3.3.2).
- **The legal line** comes last, in your words, with links to terms and privacy if you have them.
- **Another language**: the group titles, links and legal line are yours, and the pickers' words are in `labels`.

<Canvas of={Stories.LinksOnly} />

## What stays your app's job

- Changing the page's language (usually a new address) and its theme (setting `data-theme` on the page, and remembering the choice).
- Keeping the links the same on every page, so people find them where they were.
