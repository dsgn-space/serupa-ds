# Pricing

Plans side by side, billed monthly or yearly. Each plan says what it includes and has one clear action. It's built from Card, SegmentedControl, Badge and Button.

<Canvas of={Stories.Monthly} />

## When to use it

- Two to four plans that people compare and choose between, with a monthly or yearly price.
- Not for one price: say it in a sentence beside the button that buys it.
- Not for comparing many features across plans: add a Table under the plans.

## What each part is for

- **The plans are a list**, and each plan's name is a heading (1.3.1). A screen reader hears how many plans there are and can jump between them. Pick `headingLevel` so the names sit under your page's own heading: the default is 3, under an h2.
- **What a plan includes is a real list**, named "Included in Pro", so it isn't read as one long sentence. The check marks are decoration and are hidden from screen readers.
- **Monthly or yearly is one choice** (a radio group named "Billing period"). The yearly option says the saving in its name, "Yearly, save 20%", so nobody has to work it out. The saving is rounded down, so it never promises more than it gives. When the plans save different amounts, it says "save up to 30%": `labels.yearly` gets the biggest saving and whether to say "up to".
- **Yearly prices are shown per month**, with what is paid once a year written underneath, so the two periods compare fairly and the real charge isn't hidden.
- **The current plan and the recommended plan say so in words** (1.4.1). The recommended plan also has a stronger border and the primary button, but the word carries the meaning. The current plan has no button, because there's nothing to choose.
- **One action per plan, named after it**: "Choose Pro", not a row of "Choose" buttons that sound the same in a list of buttons (2.4.6). If `onChoose` returns a promise, the button shows that it's working until it settles.
- **It says the plans renew, and where to cancel, before anyone chooses.** Under the plans, a line reads "Paid plans renew every month at the price shown until you cancel" (every year when billed yearly), with a link: "Cancel any time in Billing settings". Point `cancelHref` at your billing settings. That follows the Ethical design rule: say when a plan renews and what it will cost before someone chooses it, and how to cancel. The line is left out when every plan is free.
- **Long words wrap inside the card**, such as a German "Datenschutzgrundverordnung", so nothing runs out of it at any width (1.4.10).
- **Money is written the reader's way.** Prices follow the locale: "$12" in English, "Rp 150.000" in Indonesian.
- **Another language**: every word is in `labels`, with English defaults. `yearly`, `billedYearly`, `included`, `choose` and `renews` are functions, so word order can follow the language.

## What stays your app's job

- The plans, their prices and the currency, and which plan the person is on.
- What happens after a plan is chosen: a checkout, a confirmation, or a change of plan.
- Taxes and refunds, which belong near the plans in words, and a billing settings page where cancelling takes no more steps than choosing a plan did.
