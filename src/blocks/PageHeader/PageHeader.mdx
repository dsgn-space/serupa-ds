# Page header

The top of a page inside an app: where the page is, what it's called, what it's for, and what you can do there.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- At the top of main on any page below the app's top level: a project, a customer, a report.
- With only a title and one action for a top-level page such as a list.
- Not for the app's own header with the logo and account menu: that's the Navbar, outside main.

## What each part is for

- **A Breadcrumb**, so someone who arrived from a search or a link knows where they are and can go up a level (2.4.8). Its last item is this page, not a link, and says `aria-current="page"`.
- **The title**, the page's one h1. Keep it the same as the page's `<title>` and the item it names.
- **A description**, one sentence in plain text, not a heading. Leave it out rather than repeat the title.
- **The primary action**, one button, always shown. **The secondary actions** sit beside it on a wide screen and move into a "More actions" Menu on a phone, so none is cut off or makes the page scroll sideways (1.4.10).
- **Section links**, a `nav` with its own name (`sectionsLabel`), so it's told apart from the breadcrumb and the app's navigation in a screen reader's landmark list. The current one says `aria-current="page"` and is bold, not only a different color.
- **Reading order** matches the Tab order: breadcrumb, title, primary action, other actions, sections.

## What stays your app's job

- The page's `<title>`, matching the h1.
- What each action does, and where each section link goes. Use links (`href`) for actions that go to another page, and `onSelect` for ones that act here.
- Using Tabs instead of section links when the sections are panels on this page rather than pages of their own.
