# Sign-in card

The sign-in card from the [Sign-in pattern](?path=/docs/patterns-sign-in--docs), as a block you place on your own page: email and password, then a one-time code, then signed in. The pattern is this card on a page of its own, so the two share one piece of code. It meets WCAG 2.2's accessible authentication (3.3.8).

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A sign-in page with more on it than the form: your product's name and a welcome, a picture, or links for new people.
- A page with a sign-in step inside a longer flow, such as a checkout.
- For a page that holds only the form, copy the Sign-in pattern instead. It adds the skip link, `main` and the h1.

## What each part is for

The [Sign-in pattern](?path=/docs/patterns-sign-in--docs) explains each field, the error summary, the code step and why there's no puzzle. On top of that, the card:

- **Fits under your heading.** `headingLevel` sets the card's heading: 1 when the card is the page, 2 under your own h1. The error summary's heading is one below it.
- **Waits for your server.** `check` and `verify` can return a promise. The button shows that it's working until it settles, and takes no second press.
- **Skips the code step without `verify`.** A right password then ends on "You’re signed in".
- **Tells you when it's done.** `onSignedIn` runs once, as the done step appears. `onResend` runs each time "Send a new code" is pressed.
- **Keeps its ids yours.** `idPrefix` starts each field's id, which the error summary's links point at. Without it, the ids are React's own, so two cards on a page never clash.
- **Another language**: every word is in `labels`, with English defaults, the error messages too.

## What stays your app's job

- Checking the email, password and code against your server, and sending the code.
- Rate limits, and locking an account after many tries, said in words on the page when it happens.
- Adding "Error: " to the page's `<title>` after a failed try.
- Where people go once they're signed in.

## Copying it

Copy `AuthCard.tsx` and `AuthCard.test.tsx`, change the imports to `serupa`, and pass your own `check` and `verify`.
