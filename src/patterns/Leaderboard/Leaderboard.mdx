# Leaderboard

Who's ahead this week, this month or of all time, where you are, and who's moving.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A challenge, a game or a sales team where people compare their points.

## When not to use it

- Not for a table of data to sort and filter: that's the List and search pattern or DataGrid.

## What each part is for

- **The board is a table with a caption**; each row's name is its row header, so every cell is read with it (1.3.1).
- **A tie shares its place**, said as "Joint 3rd", not left to the numbers to show.
- **Your row says "You" in a badge** as well as being shaded, and a move is said in words, "Up 2", never only an arrow or a color (1.4.1).
- **Switching the period says which period the board shows**, in a status (4.1.3).
- **When you're outside the top places**, your row follows them after a row that says how many people are between (1.3.2).
- **The summary under the title says your place**, of how many, with your points.
- **While the leaderboard loads**, set `loading`: one placeholder, named "Loading the leaderboard" (4.1.3). **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`, for its Try again.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.InTheTop} />

## What stays your app's job

- **Scoring.** Pass each person's points for each period, and how many places they moved.
- **Privacy.** Show only people who agreed to be on the board, and let people hide their name.
