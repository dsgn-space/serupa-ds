# Email preferences

The page an email's "Manage emails" or "Unsubscribe" link opens, with no sign-in: which emails come, a switch for each, and one button to stop them all.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- The link at the foot of every marketing email.

## When not to use it

- Not for alerts inside the app: that's the Notifications pattern, whose settings link goes to your own settings page.
- Not for a whole account's settings: that's the Settings form pattern.

## What each part is for

- **The address it's about is said first**, so someone with two addresses knows which one this is (1.3.1).
- **Each kind of email is a switch named by what it is**, with how often it comes as its description (1.3.1, 3.3.2).
- **Stopping every email is one button**, beside Save, with no "Are you sure?"; it takes effect at once.
- **Save shows it's busy and says what changed** once saved; one that fails says so and keeps the switches as set (4.1.3, 3.3.4).
- **After "Stop all emails", the answer's heading takes focus**, with "Turn them back on" (2.4.3).
- **The emails that can't be turned off are named in a sentence**, not shown as switches that can't be switched.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.SaveFails} />

## What stays your app's job

- **Knowing who.** The link carries a token for the address; check it on the server, and let it change only that address's emails.
- **Saving.** `onSave` gets every topic's choice: reject when they can't be saved. Stop sending within the day you say.
- **The kinds.** Pass the topics you really send, with how often; an "Unsubscribe" link in an email should turn off only that email's kind, then show this page.
