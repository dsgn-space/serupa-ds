# Booking

A stay at one place: the day the guests arrive and the day they leave, picked on two months side by side, with the nights already booked named, the price worked out, and a way to reserve.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A room, a house, a desk or a piece of kit that's booked by the night or the day, with some days already taken.

## When not to use it

- Not for times within a day, such as a meeting room by the hour: that's the Team calendar pattern's form, or a TimePicker.
- Not for paying: Reserve hands the stay to your app, which goes on to the Checkout pattern if it takes money now.

## What each part is for

- **Two months side by side**, so a stay across the end of a month is picked without paging. They stack on a narrow screen. The calendar is named by the "Your dates" heading, and names the picked days "start date" and "end date" (1.3.1).
- **Booked nights are struck through and also listed in words** under "Already booked", each written as the stay it is, so nobody has to tell closed days by their look (1.4.1).
- **A stay can end on the day a booked one starts, and start on the day one ends.** Only a day whose night and the night before are both booked is closed; the sentence under the list says so.
- **Dates that take in a booked night, or are shorter than the place allows, are said at once** in an alert under the calendar, naming the nights. The calendar points to it with aria-describedby, so it's read again from the calendar (3.3.1, 4.1.3).
- **Reserve with no dates says what to choose** and puts focus on the calendar, rather than being a disabled button that doesn't say why (3.3.1).
- **The guests field says how many the place sleeps**, and its count can't go past it (3.3.2).
- **The price is a description list**: the nightly price times the nights, the cleaning fee and the total, with the total named "Total" (1.3.1).
- **Reserve books only once the server says so.** The button shows it's busy; a booking that fails says so next to the button and keeps the dates (3.3.1, 4.1.3).
- **Once booked, the form gives way to a confirmation** whose heading takes focus and says the dates, the nights and the guests (2.4.3, 4.1.3).
- **While the place loads**, set `loading`: one placeholder, named "Loading place". **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **Booking.** `onReserve` gets the days as "yyyy-mm-dd" and the number of guests: return its promise, and reject it when the dates were taken in the meantime. Then load the place again, so the new booked nights show.
- **Today.** Pass `today` as the visitor's date, so days before it are closed.
- **The booked nights.** `taken` holds each night by the day it starts, the way a hotel counts them.
- **Prices that change by the night**, taxes and discounts: work them out on the server and show them in the same description list.
- **Paying**, and who's booking: send them on to the Checkout pattern and the Sign in pattern.
