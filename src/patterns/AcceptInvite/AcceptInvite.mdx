# Accept invite

The page a workspace invite links to: who invited you, to what and as what, and Join or Decline; or why the invite can't be used any more.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- The link in an email inviting someone into a team, a workspace or a shared project.

## When not to use it

- Not for inviting people: that's the Team members pattern.
- Not for a friend's offer with a reward: that's the Referral pattern.

## What each part is for

- **Who invited you is the first thing said, in the h1**; your role, the members and when the invite ends are named facts, so nothing hangs on a picture (1.3.1).
- **The members' pictures are one named group**, each picture named by its person, with how many in words (1.1.1).
- **The account you'd join with is said before Join**, with a way to switch; when it isn't the address the invite went to, a warning says so in words (3.3.4).
- **Join shows it's busy**; one that fails says so next to the buttons and changes nothing (3.3.4, 4.1.3).
- **After Join or Decline, the answer's heading takes focus** and says what happened, with where to go next (2.4.3).
- **An invite that has ended or been used says why in the h1**, and who to ask for a new one, rather than a bare error.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.OtherAccount} />

## What stays your app's job

- **Joining.** `onJoin` adds the signed-in account: reject when it can't. Check the invite again on the server, as it may have ended since the page loaded.
- **Signing in.** Someone without an account goes to the Sign up pattern first, with the invite's address filled in, and comes back here.
- **Ending invites.** Pass `used` once someone has joined with it, and an `expires` time; seven days is common.
