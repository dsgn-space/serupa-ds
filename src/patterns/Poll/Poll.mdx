# Poll

One question, a few answers and a vote; then the results so far, with yours marked, and a way to change it while it's open.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A quick question to a team or a community: where to go, which day, which name.

## When not to use it

- Not for many questions, or answers in words: that's the Feedback survey pattern.
- Not for scoring answers: that's the Quiz pattern.

## What each part is for

- **The answers are a group of radios named by the question** (1.3.1).
- **It says when it closes and who sees what** before you vote (3.3.2).
- **Voting without an answer shows a summary that takes focus**, its link landing on the first answer (3.3.1, 2.4.3).
- **Vote shows it's busy**; a vote that can't be sent says so and keeps your answer (3.3.4, 4.1.3).
- **Once voted, the results' heading takes focus** (2.4.3). The results are a list; each answer is a meter whose value is in words, "15 votes, 50%", never only a bar's length (1.3.1, 1.4.1). Yours says "Your vote".
- **The shares add up to 100**: the answers with the largest remainders get the leftover points.
- **Change your vote** brings back the answers with yours chosen; Keep my vote goes back without sending.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **Counting.** `onVote` gets the answer and the one it replaces, and resolves with the new counts; reject when it can't be sent. Without it, the page counts on its own, for a demo.
- **One vote each**, and keeping who voted for what private when you say it is.
- **Closing it** on the day it says, then showing it `closed`.
- **"Error: " in the page's `<title>`** while the error summary shows, so a reload says it first (3.3.1).
