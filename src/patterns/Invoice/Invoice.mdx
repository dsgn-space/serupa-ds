# Invoice

One bill, as the person paying it reads it: whether it's due, paid or overdue, who it's from and to, its lines, the totals, and a way to pay or keep a copy.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- The page an invoice email links to, or a row in a list of invoices opens.

## When not to use it

- Not for the list of invoices and the plan they come from: that's the Billing pattern.
- Not for paying for a basket: that's the Checkout pattern.

## What each part is for

- **Where it stands is a word and a sentence**: "Due" in a badge, then "Due on March 31, 2025", or "Overdue by 5 days. It was due on March 3, 2025". The badge's color is never the only sign (1.4.1).
- **The amount comes first**, as the page's first h2, with what to do with it ("to pay").
- **The lines are a table** with a caption and column headers, the amounts aligned at the end, written the way the reader's locale writes money (1.3.1).
- **The totals are a description list**, ending with the amount due, named so (1.3.1).
- **Dates are the reader's own**, written out in full, with a date a machine can read.
- **Download says what it downloads**: "Download PDF of invoice INV-2041", starting with the words it shows (2.4.4, 2.5.3).
- **Pay marks it paid only once the server says so.** The button shows it's busy; a payment that fails says so next to it, and says nothing was taken (3.3.1, 4.1.3).
- **Once paid, the Pay button is gone**, so focus goes to the h1; the badge says "Paid" and a toast says so (2.4.3, 4.1.3).
- **While the invoice loads**, set `loading`: one placeholder, named "Loading invoice". **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **Paying.** `onPay` gets the amount due: take the payment there, and reject its promise when it fails. For a card, send the reader to your payment provider's page from there.
- **The PDF.** `pdf` is its address; make it on the server, with the same lines and totals.
- **Today.** Pass `today` as the reader's date, so overdue is right.
- **Tax rules.** One rate on the whole invoice is all the pattern does; work out mixed rates on the server.
