# Profile

What other people see of you: a photo, your name, a short bio, where you are and a link, edited in one form and saved when you say so.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A person's own public details in an app with other people in it: a community, a marketplace, a team.

## When not to use it

- Not for account details like email, password and two-step sign-in: those are the Account security pattern.
- Not for preferences that save as you change them: that's the Settings form pattern.

## What each part is for

- **The form's heading says whose profile it is**: "Edit Ana Lima's profile" (2.4.6).
- **The photo has a name**: "Photo of Ana Lima". Without one, the avatar shows initials and still names you (1.1.1).
- **Each field says its limits**: the photo which files and how big, the bio its length, counted as you type in characters as people see them (3.3.2).
- **Name, location and website say what they are for autofill** (1.3.5).
- **Saving with problems shows a summary at the top of the form that takes focus**, each a link to its field, and each field says its own: a missing name, or a link that isn't one. A photo that's too big or not an image is said by the upload itself (3.3.1).
- **Save says where it's got to**: Saving, Saved with when, or a failure with Try again, in a status that's read out. A save that fails says so and keeps your changes (4.1.3).
- **Discard changes shows only once something has changed**, and puts back what was saved (3.3.4).
- **The preview shows what others see** as you type, so nobody has to save to check.
- **While the profile loads**, set `loading`: one placeholder, named "Loading profile". **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **Saving.** `onSave` gets the profile and the new photo's file, or null: return its promise, and reject it when it can't be saved. Upload the photo there, and send its new address back as `profile.photo` next time.
- **Cropping the photo.** Make it square on the server, or before `onSave` sends it.
- **Who sees what.** Say on the page if some of it is private.
- **"Error: " in the page's `<title>`** while the error summary shows, so a reload says it first (3.3.1).
