# Status page

Whether it's working now, each service's last 90 days, what's being done about a problem, and updates by email.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A public page people check when something seems broken, on its own domain so it stays up when the product doesn't.

## When not to use it

- Not for a banner in the product: that's an Alert, or the Offline banner.

## What each part is for

- **The overall state leads, in words**, as the title of an alert: "Some things are slow or partly working" (1.3.1).
- **Each service is a heading with its state as a word in a badge**, never a color alone, and its 90 days as a StatusTracker that names each day in words (1.4.1).
- **Each incident is an article named by its heading**, saying what it affects; its updates are an ordered list, newest first, each with its time in a `<time>` (1.3.1, 2.4.6).
- **Subscribing with a wrong email says so next to the field** and keeps focus there; once subscribed, a status says where updates go (3.3.1, 4.1.3).
- **While the services load**, set `loading`: one placeholder, named "Loading the status" (4.1.3). **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`, for its Try again.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **Knowing the state.** Pass each service's state and days, and the incidents with their updates, from your monitoring.
- **Updates by email.** `onSubscribe` gets the address; reject when it can't be added. Confirm it with an email before sending updates.
- **Hosting it** apart from the product, so it loads when the product doesn't.
