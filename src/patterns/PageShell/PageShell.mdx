# Page shell

The frame around an app's pages: a skip link, a sticky Navbar, a Sidebar beside main, and a footer.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- An app with several pages that people move between, where the navigation stays put and only the content changes.
- Not for a single page or a sign-in screen: give it its own simple layout with a skip link.

## What each part is for

- **The SkipLink** is the first Tab stop. It moves focus into main, past the Navbar and the Sidebar (WCAG 2.4.1).
- **The Navbar** is the banner, and sticky. Its scroll padding keeps a focused element in main below the bar (2.4.11).
- **The Sidebar** is a second navigation landmark, so it gets its own name ("Workspace"), told apart from the Navbar's. On a phone it gives its room to main, and its links move into the Navbar's menu, under "Workspace" (a NavbarMenu shown only on a phone), so none of them is lost.
- **Collapsed**, the Sidebar is an icon rail that gives main more room. Each item keeps its name, read out and shown as a tooltip.
- **main** holds the page's one h1.
- **The footer** is the contentinfo landmark.

## Copying it

Copy the story's `render` into your app's layout, change the imports to `serupa`, and put each page's content in main.
