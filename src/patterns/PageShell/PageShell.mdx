# Page shell

The frame around an app's pages: a skip link, a sticky Navbar, a Sidebar beside main, and a footer, laid out by [AppShell](?path=/docs/components-layout-and-type-appshell--docs) with no CSS of its own.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- An app with several pages that people move between, where the navigation stays put and only the content changes.

## When not to use it

- Not for a single page or a sign-in screen: give it its own simple layout with a skip link.

## What each part is for

- **The SkipLink** is the first Tab stop. It moves focus into main, past the Navbar and the Sidebar (WCAG 2.4.1).
- **The Navbar** is the banner, and sticky. Its scroll padding keeps a focused element in main below the bar (2.4.11).
- **The Sidebar** is a second navigation landmark, so it gets its own name ("Workspace"), told apart from the Navbar's. On a phone it gives its room to main, and its links move into the Navbar's menu, under "Workspace" (a NavbarMenu shown only on a phone), so none of them is lost.
- **Collapsed**, the Sidebar is an icon rail that gives main more room. Each item keeps its name, read out and shown as a tooltip.
- **main** holds the page's one h1.
- **The footer** is the contentinfo landmark.

<Canvas of={Stories.CollapsedSidebar} />

**Adaptive**, the navigation follows the window's width: a BottomNav below 768px, the Sidebar's icon rail from 768px, and the full Sidebar from 1024px, with the same three places in the same order at every width. Page layout's "Navigation by width" says when to use which.

## Keeping state

When people come back to a page, show it as they left it, so they never retrace their steps. Decide by who should see it:

- **In the URL**, for what someone may share or bookmark: the open tab, a search, filters, sort, the page of a list. Read it on load and write it on change, with the components controlled: Tabs `value` and `onValueChange`, FilterBar `value` and `onValueChange`, Table's `sort` and `onSort` on each TableHeaderCell, Pagination's `page` and `onPageChange`.
- **In storage** (localStorage, or the person's settings on your server when it should follow them to another device), for their own layout: the Sidebar's `collapsed`, column widths (TableHeaderCell `width` and `onWidthChange`), which Accordion sections are open (`value` and `onValueChange`). Splitter keeps its sizes itself with `storageKey`, and KeyboardShortcuts keeps the single-key setting itself.
- **Nowhere**, for what's over once it closes: an open Menu, Popover or Dialog, a hover, a toast. Opening a page with a dialog already open surprises people.

Wrap every storage read in try/catch, since it can be blocked, and fall back to the default.

## What stays your app's job

- **The navigation.** Pass your own sections and links, and mark the one for the page people are on.
- **Each page's content**, inside main, with its own h1.

## Copying it

Copy the story's `render` into your app's layout, change the imports to `serupa`, and put each page's content in main.
