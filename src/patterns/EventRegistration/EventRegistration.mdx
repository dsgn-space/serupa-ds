# Event registration

When and where an event is, how many places are left, and a short form to get tickets, or to join the waiting list once it's full.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- An event's own page: a meetup, a workshop, a class, with a set number of places.

## When not to use it

- Not for booking a time with a person: that's the Appointment pattern.
- Not for a stay or a table over a range of dates: that's the Booking pattern.

## What each part is for

- **When and where are named facts**, and the time is shown in the event's own time zone, with its name, so nobody turns up an hour out (1.3.1).
- **Places left are a meter with the number in words**, "13 places left", not a bar alone (1.4.1).
- **Each ticket says its price and who it's for**; how many tickets says its limit, and the total is worked out as you choose (3.3.2).
- **Name and email say what they are for autofill**, and what would make it easier to come is asked for, optional (1.3.5).
- **Registering with problems shows a summary at the top that takes focus**, each problem a link to its field (3.3.1, 2.4.3).
- **Register shows it's busy**; a registration that can't be sent says so next to the button and keeps every field (3.3.4, 4.1.3).
- **Once registered, the answer's heading takes focus**, with the tickets, the total and a calendar file to add it (2.4.3).
- **Once it's full, the form becomes a waiting list**, saying what happens when a place comes free.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.Full} />

## What stays your app's job

- **Registering.** `onRegister` gets the registration, trimmed: reject when it can't be sent. Hold the places on the server, as two people can take the last one at once.
- **Paying.** For a paid ticket, go on to the Checkout pattern with the total.
- **The time zone.** Give the event's `timeZone` and a name for it people know; the page shows the times there, wherever it's read.
- **"Error: " in the page's `<title>`** while the error summary shows, so a reload says it first (3.3.1).
