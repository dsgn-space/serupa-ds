# Send money

Choose who, say how much, see what they'll get, check it all on a review step, then send.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- Sending money from an account to someone you've paid before, in their currency.

## When not to use it

- Not for paying a bill or a shop: that's the Checkout pattern.
- Not for adding someone new: that's its own form, with their bank details checked first.

## What each part is for

- **Who it's to is a group of radios named by the question**, each with their bank as its description (1.3.1).
- **Your balance is said before you type**, and what they'll get, the fee and the rate follow the amount (3.3.2, 4.1.3).
- **Continuing with problems shows a summary at the top that takes focus**, each problem a link; an amount over your balance says so with the fee counted (3.3.1, 2.4.3).
- **Money moves only after a review step** that lists who, how much, the fee, what they get and when it arrives (3.3.4). Change goes back with everything kept.
- **Each step's heading takes focus**: the review, the form again on Change, and the answer once sent (2.4.3).
- **Send names the total and shows it's busy**; a transfer that can't be sent says nothing moved and stays on the review (3.3.4, 4.1.3).
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.SendFails} />

## What stays your app's job

- **Moving the money.** `onSend` gets who, the amount, the fee, what they get and the reference; reject when it can't be sent. Lock the rate for the review step and say for how long.
- **Checking it's them.** Ask for a second step, such as a code, when your rules or the amount call for it.
- **The rates and fees.** Pass each recipient's `rate` and `arrives`, and your `fee`.
- **"Error: " in the page's `<title>`** while the error summary shows, so a reload says it first (3.3.1).
