# Support ticket

One request to a support team, as the person who asked sees it: where it's got to, the conversation so far, and a reply box, or a way to reopen it once solved.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- The page a "We've replied to your request" email links to.

## When not to use it

- Not for writing in the first time: that's the Contact form pattern.
- Not for a live chat: that's the Messages pattern.

## What each part is for

- **Where it's got to is a word in a badge**, "Waiting on you", with what that means in a sentence, not a color alone. The reference, when it was opened and its priority are named facts (1.4.1, 1.3.1).
- **The conversation is a list, oldest first**; each message is an article named by who wrote it and when, and says "Support" or "You" in words, besides its picture (1.3.1).
- **The reply says what it takes**; an empty one is named next to the box, which takes focus (3.3.1, 3.3.2).
- **Send shows it's busy**; a sent reply joins the list and a toast says so; one that can't be sent says so and keeps the words and the files (4.1.3, 3.3.4).
- **Mark as solved moves focus to the heading that says it's solved**, with Reopen; Reopen brings the reply box back and focuses it (2.4.3).
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.Solved} />

## What stays your app's job

- **Sending.** `onReply` gets the words, trimmed, and the files: reject when it can't be sent.
- **Where it's got to.** Move it to "Waiting on you" when an agent asks something, and back to "Open" when the person replies, as the page does.
- **Solving.** `onSolve` and `onReopen` hear the person's choice; say how long a solved request can be reopened.
