# Sign-up

A new account in two short steps: name, email, an optional phone, a password and the terms, then a code from the email to show it's theirs.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- Anyone can make an account, and you need to know the email reaches them.
- Not for an invited person joining a team: their email is known, so ask only for a name and a password.
- Not when a sign-in provider (Google, Apple, your company's) is the main way in: lead with that, and keep this form as the other way.

## What each part is for

- **Ask for little.** Name, email and a password. The phone is marked optional and says what it's for. Every other detail can wait for Settings.
- **Each field says what it holds** (`name`, `email`, `tel`, `new-password`, `one-time-code`), so the browser and a password manager fill it in and offer a strong password (1.3.5).
- **The password's rules are shown before typing** (3.3.2), and each says "done" or "not yet" in words as well as by its mark. The rules are about length and not reusing the email, not a mix of symbols, which makes passwords harder to remember and no stronger.
- **Show password** lets people check what they typed, instead of typing it twice.
- **The terms** are a checkbox the person ticks, never ticked for them, with links to read them.
- **Create account with problems** shows an Error summary that takes focus and links to each field, and each field says what to do (3.3.1, 3.3.3).
- **The code step** says where the code went and for how long it works. The code field fills from the email on a phone, and pasting works (3.3.8).
- **Change email** goes back with every answer kept, so nothing is typed twice (3.3.7).
- **Each new step puts focus on its heading**, so a screen reader says where they are (2.4.3).

## What stays the app's job

- **Creating the account and sending the code.** `onSubmit` gets the answers once they pass the checks: return its promise, and the button shows it's busy. Reject it to stay on the form, and say why.
- **Checking the code.** `verify` stands in for your server here. Offer "Send a new code" once a little time has passed.
- **An email that already has an account.** Don't say so on this page, which would tell anyone who has an account. Send that address an email instead.
- **The rules themselves.** Keep the minimum length at 12 or more, and check new passwords against lists of leaked ones on the server.
