# Account security

Changing the password, turning two-step verification on and off, the devices signed in, each with Sign out, and deleting the account.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- The security page of any account people sign in to.

## When not to use it

- Not for signing in or signing up: those are their own patterns, and this page is reached once signed in.
- Not for an admin managing other people's accounts: that's Team members.

## What each part is for

- **The password fields say what they hold**, `current-password` and `new-password`, so a password manager fills the first and suggests the second, and paste works (3.3.8, 1.3.5). Show password is on each.
- **The rules come first.** "At least 12 characters" is in the hint before anyone types, not only in an error after a failed try (3.3.2). Length beats symbols, so there are no rules about character types.
- **Problems are listed** in an error summary that takes focus and links to each field: a missing current password, a short new one, or the same one again (3.3.1). A save that fails says so with Try again, and keeps what was typed.
- **Two-step setup works without a camera.** The key is shown as text with Copy, so it can be typed or pasted into the app, and the code box takes a pasted code (3.3.8). A wrong code says what to check, the phone's clock, and the boxes clear with focus in the first, ready for the next code.
- **Recovery codes come before setup ends**, once, with Copy, so nobody is locked out by a lost phone (3.3.4). Two-step is on from the moment the code works; closing the codes says so in a toast.
- **Turning two-step off asks first**, in a dialog that says what's left protecting the account, with Keep it on beside Turn off.
- **Each device says where and when**, "Bandung, Indonesia, last active 6 days ago", and each Sign out is named with its device, "Sign out of Firefox on a Windows PC" (2.4.6). The device in use says "This device" in words and can't be signed out here.
- **Signing out is immediate**: a session someone may not recognize shouldn't wait for an Undo. Focus goes to the next device's Sign out, or the list's heading, and a toast says it's done (2.4.3, 4.1.3). Sign out everywhere else shows when two or more other devices are signed in.
- **The devices can load late or fail on their own.** While they load, their list is busy with placeholder rows, named once ("Loading where you’re signed in"); a failed load says so in their place, with Try again (4.1.3, 3.3.3). The password, two-step and delete parts work meanwhile.
- **Deleting the account sits apart, at the end**, and says what goes. Download your data comes first, beside it, so nobody has to choose between leaving and keeping their work. The dialog asks for the account's email before Delete my account works (Type to confirm), and pasting it is fine (3.3.4, 3.3.8). Leaving takes no more steps than signing up did: no survey, no hidden link.
- **Give people time to change their mind.** With `deleteAfter`, Delete my account schedules the delete instead: the dialog says the day ("Your projects, files and settings will be deleted for good on 28 November 2026"), and until then a banner at the top of the page says it again with Keep my account, which takes focus as the dialog closes (3.3.4, 2.4.3). Delete account is gone from its section meanwhile, so there's one way back, not two. Pass `deletionDate` when the person comes back before the day, and the page opens with the banner.
- **A paid plan says what happens to it.** With `plan`, the section and the dialog say "Your Pro plan stops renewing, so you won’t be charged again", so nobody deletes the account wondering about the next bill.
- **A delete that fails says so.** The dialog stays open with an alert, "Couldn’t delete your account. Try again." (3.3.1, 4.1.3). After the delete, the page says the account is gone, with its h1 focused (2.4.3).
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **Checking and storing.** `onChangePassword` gets the current and new password: return its promise, and reject it if the current one is wrong or saving fails. `onEnableTwoStep` gets the code: reject it if the code is wrong. `onDisableTwoStep`, `onSignOut` and `onSignOutOthers` get those choices. `onDeleteAccount` gets the delete: return its promise, and the button shows it's busy until it settles.
- **Loading the devices.** Pass `loading` while they're on their way, `loadFailed` if they didn't come, and `onRetry` to load them again: return its promise, and Try again shows it's working until it settles.
- **The setup key and recovery codes**, as `setupKey` and `recoveryCodes`. Generate them on the server, and add a QR code of the key next to the text if you like.
- **Telling people by email** when the password changes or two-step is turned off, so someone else's change doesn't go unseen.
- **The data export** that `dataHref` leads to, and what happens to shared work when someone leaves.
- **Doing the scheduled delete.** `onDeleteAccount` gets the day as "2026-11-28" when `deleteAfter` is set: delete then, email the person when it's done, and stop the plan's renewal at once. `onKeepAccount` gets Keep my account: return its promise, and reject it if it couldn’t be kept: the banner then says so and stays. Refunds for the rest of a paid period follow your own terms.
- Rendering a `Toaster` once in the app.
- **"Error: " in the page's `<title>`** while the error summary shows, so a reload says it first (3.3.1).
