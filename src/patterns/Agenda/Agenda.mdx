# Conference agenda

The sessions of each day by time, filtered by track, saved to your own agenda, with clashes said.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A conference's or a festival's programme, where people pick what to go to.

## When not to use it

- Not for booking a time with someone: that's the Appointment pattern.
- Not for a team's calendar: that's the Team calendar pattern.

## What each part is for

- **Each day is a tab**, and your agenda is the last one, with how many you've saved (1.3.1).
- **Each start time is a heading** over the sessions that start then; each session is an article named by its title, with who, when, where and its track as a word (1.3.1, 2.4.6).
- **Save is a toggle button named by its session**, "Save: Forms that forgive", pressed or not; saving says how many are in your agenda (4.1.2, 4.1.3).
- **A saved session that overlaps another says which**, in words, on both (3.3.1).
- **The track filter says how many sessions it shows**, in a status (4.1.3).
- **While the sessions load**, set `loading`: one placeholder, named "Loading the agenda" (4.1.3). **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`, for its Try again.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.Clash} />

## What stays your app's job

- **The sessions.** Pass each one's day, start and end in the event's time zone, and say which zone it is.
- **Keeping the agenda.** `onSavedChange` hears every change; save it to the person's account, and offer it as a calendar file.
