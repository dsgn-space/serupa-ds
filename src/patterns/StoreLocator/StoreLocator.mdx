# Store locator

Find a shop by town or postcode, or by where you are, with each one's distance, whether it's open now, its hours and directions.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A "Find a shop" page, linked from the footer, delivery choices and returns.

## When not to use it

- Not for booking a time at a shop: that's the Appointment pattern.
- Not for an address of your own: that's the Address book pattern.

## What each part is for

- **The search is a labeled landmark**, and the results are a list, each shop named by its heading (1.3.1, 2.4.6).
- **"Open now" or "Closed now" is a word in a badge**, with when it next opens or closes, not a color (1.4.1).
- **How many shops match, and in what order, is said in a status line** as the search or the filter changes (4.1.3).
- **When the browser can't give your location**, it says so next to the button, and what to do instead (3.3.1).
- **Each Directions link's name ends with its shop**, "Directions: Acme Kemang" (2.5.3).
- **The list works without a map**, at any zoom and with any screen reader; a map is an extra beside it.
- **While the stores load**, set `loading`: one placeholder, named "Loading stores" (4.1.3). **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`, for its Try again.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **The shops.** Pass each one's place, hours (Sunday first) and the words people search it by: town, district, postcode. Open or closed is worked out on the device's clock: for shops in another time zone, pass `now` in theirs.
- **Where someone is.** `locate` asks the browser by default; ask only when they choose "Use my location".
- **A map.** Add one beside the list if it helps, with the same shops; never instead of it.
