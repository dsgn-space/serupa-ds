# Transactions

An account's money in and out by month, found by what it was or its category, with the month's totals first.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A bank, a wallet or a card account where people check what came in and what went out.

## When not to use it

- Not for a business's ledger with many columns to sort: that's DataGrid.

## What each part is for

- **The transactions are a table** with a caption that names the month. Each one's description is its row header, so every amount is read with it (1.3.1).
- **Money in and money out are their own columns**, never a color or a minus sign alone, and a pending one says "Pending" in a badge (1.4.1).
- **The month's totals come first**, as a list of terms: in, out, and what you kept or spent beyond it (1.3.1).
- **Searching or filtering says how many show**, of how many that month, and Clear filters puts both back (4.1.3, 3.3.2).
- **At narrow widths the table scrolls on its own**, and the page doesn't (1.4.10).
- **While the transactions load**, set `loading`: one placeholder, named "Loading transactions" (4.1.3). **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`, for its Try again.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **Fetching the transactions.** Pass them with each one's date, and the amount as negative for money out.
- **Older months.** Load them as people pick them, and offer a statement to download.
