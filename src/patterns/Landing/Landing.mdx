# Landing page

The page a product shows people who don't use it yet: what it is, the one thing to do, what it does, what it costs, and the questions people ask before they start.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- The home page of a product's website, or the page an ad or a launch post links to.

## When not to use it

- Not inside the product, once someone has signed in: start them on their work, as the Dashboard pattern does.
- Not for a page with one form on it, such as a waiting list: that's a Sign up page with a line about the product.

## What each part is for

- **The h1 says what the product does for the person**, in their words, not its name or a slogan. The line under it says who it's for. Together they are the page's outline in a list of headings (1.3.1, 2.4.6).
- **One call to action, said the same everywhere.** "Start free trial" is in the header, the hero and the last section, with the same words and doing the same thing, so it's known again each time (3.2.4). It says where it leads (2.4.4), and the line under it says what trying costs. The second button only moves down the page.
- **What it does** is a list of features, each a heading with a sentence on what it means for the person. The icons are decoration, so they aren't read out (1.1.1).
- **Pricing** is the Pricing block: monthly or yearly, the saving in words, one button per plan, and that paid plans renew until canceled.
- **Questions** is the FAQ block, with the answers people need before buying (what happens when the trial ends, leaving with their data), and a way to ask something else.
- **Each section is a region named by its heading**, and the header's links go to them, so the page can be moved through by landmark or heading.
- **A skip link** first, into main, and the Footer block last, with its link groups as named navigation.
- **It reflows to one column** on a phone, and nothing is in a fixed height, so text at 200% or a 320px screen still fits (1.4.10, 1.4.4).

## What stays your app's job

- **Signing up.** `onStart` is called by every "Start free trial" button: open your sign-up, or pass the plan along when it came from Pricing's `onChoose`.
- **The words.** `headline`, `lead`, `features`, `plans`, `questions` and `footer` are yours; the defaults are a demo. Write the headline as what changes for the person, and keep the feature list short.
- **Pictures.** A screenshot or illustration of the product goes after the hero's buttons, with alt text that says what it shows; leave it out rather than add one that says nothing.
