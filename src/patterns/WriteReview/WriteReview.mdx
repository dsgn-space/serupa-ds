# Write a review

Stars, a few words and, if you like, photos, for something you bought; then a thank-you that says when it will show.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- The page a "How was your order?" email links to, or Write a review on an order.

## When not to use it

- Not for a quick rating of a service: that's the Feedback survey pattern.
- Not for showing reviews: that's the Product page pattern.

## What each part is for

- **What's being reviewed is named first**, with its picture and when it was bought (1.3.1).
- **The stars are a group of radios named by the question**, each star named by its number (1.3.1).
- **The review says how long it needs to be and what helps others**, and the name says how it will show (3.3.2).
- **Sending with problems shows a summary at the top that takes focus**, each problem a link to its field; the stars' link lands on a star (3.3.1, 2.4.3).
- **Send shows it's busy**; a review that can't be sent says so and keeps everything, the photos too (3.3.4, 4.1.3).
- **Once sent, the thank-you's heading takes focus**, and says reviews are read before they show, and when (2.4.3).
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.SendFails} />

## What stays your app's job

- **Sending.** `onSend` gets the review, trimmed, with the photos as files: reject when it can't be sent.
- **Reading them.** Check reviews before they show, and say so; never leave out a review only because it's low.
- **Who can review.** Only people who bought it, once each; say "Bought" so readers know.
- **"Error: " in the page's `<title>`** while the error summary shows, so a reload says it first (3.3.1).
