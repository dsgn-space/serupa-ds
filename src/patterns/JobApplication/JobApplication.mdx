# Job application

What a job is, one short form to apply with a CV, and what happens next.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A job's own page on a careers site, with the form under the job's facts.
- Not for a long form in several steps, such as a grant or a visa: that's the Multi-step form pattern.
- Not for a quick question to a team: that's the Contact form pattern.

## What each part is for

- **The job's facts come first, as a list of named facts**: team, place, hours and pay, so they're read before applying (1.3.1).
- **Each field says what it's for**, which are optional, and its format; name, email and phone say what they are for autofill. The CV says the file types and size it takes (3.3.2, 1.3.5).
- **Links to their work are a FieldList**, optional: a group named "Links to your work" whose rows are named "Link 1", "Link 2". Add a link puts focus in the new row and stops at 3, saying why; a link that isn't a full address is named in the summary by its number, and blank rows aren't sent (1.3.1, 4.1.3, 3.3.1).
- **Sending with problems shows a summary at the top that takes focus**, each problem a link to its field: the CV's lands on its Choose button, the question's on its first answer. Each field says its own problem too (3.3.1, 2.4.3).
- **Send shows it's busy**; an application that can't be sent says so next to the button and keeps every field and the CV (3.3.4, 4.1.3).
- **Once sent, the answer's heading takes focus**, with what happens next, in order, and a promise to answer either way (2.4.3).
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **Sending.** `onApply` gets the application, trimmed, with the CV as a `File`: reject when it can't be sent.
- **The questions.** Ask only what you need to decide on a call; each extra question loses people. Copy the pattern and change them.
- **Keeping it.** Say how long you keep an application and why in the privacy notice, and delete it when that time is up.
- **"Error: " in the page's `<title>`** while the error summary shows, so a reload says it first (3.3.1).
