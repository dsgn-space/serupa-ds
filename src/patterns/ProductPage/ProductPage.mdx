# Product page

One product in a shop: its pictures, its price, the choices to make (a color, a size), how many, and Add to cart, which says what went in.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A page that sells one thing, where the shopper chooses a variant before adding it to the cart.

## When not to use it

- Not for many products at once: that's the Product grid block, whose cards link here.
- Not for the cart or paying: that's the Cart summary block and the Checkout pattern.

## What each part is for

- **Every picture has its own alt text**, which says what that picture shows that the others don't: "The back, with a box pleat under the collar", not the product's name three times (1.1.1). The pictures are a Carousel, which never moves on its own. View full size opens a Lightbox at the picture showing; arrows go through the rest, and closing it leaves the Carousel on the last one seen.
- **A sale says "Was" in words** beside the old price, with the saving in a badge. A strike-through alone isn't read out, and many people miss it (1.3.1, 1.4.1).
- **The rating is one image named by its value**, "Rating, 4.5 out of 5", and the number of reviews beside it is a link to them (1.1.1, 2.4.4).
- **Each choice is a group of radios named by its legend**, so a screen reader says "Size, M, 2 of 4" (1.3.1, 4.1.2). Cards make each one a large target (2.5.8).
- **A sold-out choice stays in its group.** It can't be picked and says "Sold out" in words, so the shopper sees that XL exists and that it's gone, not a shirt that never came in XL (1.4.1).
- **Nothing is picked for the shopper**, unless a group has only one choice in stock. A size picked by default is how people buy the wrong size.
- **Add to cart with a choice missing says which**: "Choose a size." next to that group, and focus goes to its first radio (3.3.1, 3.3.3). The button is never disabled while a choice is missing: a disabled button can't say what it's waiting for.
- **The quantity keeps to what's in stock**, and says how many are left when it's five or fewer (3.3.2, 3.3.4).
- **Adding says what was added, in full.** The button shows it's busy, then a toast says "Added to your cart: 2 × Linen shirt, Sand, M", and the cart link in the header carries the new count in its name (4.1.3).
- **An add that fails says so above the button** and keeps every choice.
- **None left says "Sold out"** in place of the choices and the button.
- **The product's name is the h1**; About this product and Reviews are h2 sections, and each review's title an h3 (1.3.1, 2.4.6).
- **While the product loads**, set `loading`: placeholders named once ("Loading product"). **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **The cart.** `onAddToCart` gets the choice for each option and the quantity: return its promise, and reject it when adding fails. Pass the cart's count as `cartCount`.
- **Stock per combination.** Here a choice is sold out on its own. When Navy is gone only in M, mark M sold out once Navy is picked, by passing new `options`.
- **Prices per variant.** When a choice changes the price, pass the new `price`, and say the change in a status so it isn't missed.
- **The pictures and their alt text.** Write each alt for what that picture adds. When a color is picked, move the Carousel to that color's picture.
- **The page's title**: "Linen shirt – Acme".
- Rendering a `Toaster` once in the app.
