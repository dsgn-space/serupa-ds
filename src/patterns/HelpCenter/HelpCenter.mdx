# Help center

The topics people come for, the common questions with a search, and a way to write to a person that says when they'll answer.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- The help page of a product: the place "Help" in the menu leads to.
- Not for help on one field or step: that's Contextual help, next to it.
- Not for a whole documentation site with many articles: link its topics from here instead.

## What each part is for

- **The first line says both ways in**: find an answer, or write to us, and how soon a person answers.
- **Topics** are cards whose heading is a link named by the topic, with what it covers under it, so a list of links reads as "Billing", "Your data", not "Read more" (2.4.4). The whole card takes the click.
- **The questions** are the FAQ block: grouped, searchable with a count that's said, and each one linkable. Its "Still stuck?" leads to the form, so no answer is a dead end (3.3.5).
- **Writing to a person is always last, in the same place** (3.2.6), and the form says who reads it, how soon, and where the answer goes before anyone writes.
- **The form says what to write** in its hint. A missing topic or message is named next to its field, and focus goes to the first (3.3.1, 3.3.2). A problem goes as soon as it's fixed.
- **A send that fails says so**, and every word stays (3.3.1). A sent message replaces the form with what happens next, with its heading focused (4.1.3, 2.4.3).
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **Sending the message.** `onSend` gets the topic and the message: return its promise, and the button shows it's busy until it settles; if it rejects, the form says it wasn't sent.
- **The articles** the topics lead to, and the questions, as `topics` and `questions`. Write questions the way people ask them.
- **The promise.** Set `replyTime` to what your team really does, and keep it.
