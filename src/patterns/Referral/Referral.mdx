# Referral

Invite friends with your own link or by email, and see who joined and what you've earned.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- An account page for a give-and-get offer: a friend gets something off, and so do you.

## When not to use it

- Not for inviting people into a workspace: that's the Team members pattern.
- Not for sharing one document or page: that's the Share dialog pattern.

## What each part is for

- **The rules are said in a sentence first**: what each of you gets, and when.
- **The totals are a row of named numbers**: invited, joined and earned.
- **The link is a read-only field labeled "Your invite link"**, with a Copy button that names what it copies and says "Copied" when it has (1.3.1, 4.1.3).
- **The email box says how to separate addresses**, and a bad one, or one already invited, is named next to it before anything is sent (3.3.2, 3.3.1).
- **Send invites shows it's busy**, and a toast says who was invited; a send that fails says so and keeps the addresses (3.3.4, 4.1.3).
- **Who you've invited is a table** with a caption, and where each is, "Invited", "Joined" or "Earned you $10", is a word in a badge, not a color (1.3.1, 1.4.1).
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.NoInvites} />

## What stays your app's job

- **Sending invites.** `onInvite` gets the addresses, lowercased and once each: reject when they can't be sent. Send each one email, and never add them to a list.
- **The link.** Make one per person, and count who joins through it on the server.
- **The rules.** Say what each side gets, when, and any limit, in the sentence at the top.
