# Expense report

Add what you spent, one expense at a time, see them in a table with the total, and send the report to be paid back.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- Claiming back what was spent on a trip or for work.

## When not to use it

- Not for paying a bill: that's the Invoice pattern.
- Not for time off: that's the Leave request pattern.

## What each part is for

- **Each field says what it takes**; the amount says its currency, and the receipt says when it's needed and what files it takes (3.3.2).
- **Adding with problems shows a summary at the top of the form that takes focus**, each problem a link to its field, and each field says its own problem too (3.3.1, 2.4.3).
- **An expense added or removed is said**, with the new total (4.1.3).
- **The expenses are a table with a caption and the total in its footer**; one with no receipt says "Receipt needed" in words in a badge (1.3.1, 1.4.1).
- **Each Remove names its expense**, "Remove: Taxi"; removing moves focus to the next row's Remove, or the form's first field when none are left (2.5.3, 2.4.3).
- **Send for approval is checked first**: no expenses, or receipts missing, are said next to the button before anything is sent; once sent, the answer's heading takes focus (3.3.4, 2.4.3).
- **While the expenses load**, set `loading`: one placeholder, named "Loading expenses" (4.1.3). **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`, for its Try again.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.ReceiptMissing} />

## What stays your app's job

- **Sending.** `onSubmit` gets the expenses: reject when the report can't be sent. Keep each receipt's file with its expense.
- **The rules.** `receiptOver` is the amount above which a receipt is needed; change the categories to your own.
- **Saving as you go.** Keep the expenses on the server as they're added, so a report can be finished another day.
- **"Error: " in the page's `<title>`** while the error summary shows, so a reload says it first (3.3.1).
