# Reviews

The average and how the stars split, then the reviews themselves, filtered by stars and sorted, a few at a time, each one marked helpful or not.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use it

- A product's reviews page, or the reviews section of a product page.

## When not to use it

- Not for writing one: that's the Write a review pattern.
- Not for a team's poll: that's the Poll pattern.

## What each part is for

- **The average is stars named in words**, "3.8 out of 5", with how many reviews it's from beside it (1.1.1).
- **The split is a list of meters**, each named by its stars, its value in words: "2 reviews, 40%" (1.3.1, 1.4.1).
- **Each review is an article named by its title**, with its stars, who wrote it, when, and "Bought it" as a word (1.3.1, 2.4.6).
- **Changing the filter or the order says how many reviews it shows**, in a status (4.1.3).
- **Helpful is a toggle button named by the review it's for**, "Helpful: Zip broke in a month", pressed or not; the count beside it follows (4.1.2, 2.5.3).
- **Show more reviews moves focus to the first one it adds** (2.4.3).
- **While the reviews load**, set `loading`: one placeholder, named "Loading reviews" (4.1.3). **A load that fails says so**: set `loadFailed` and pass `onRetry`, for its Try again.
- **A skip link** first, into main, as on every page.

## What stays your app's job

- **The reviews.** Load them, and for many, sort and filter on the server; pass `reviews`.
- **Counting Helpful.** `onHelpful` hears each press; keep one per person.
- **Reading them before they show**, and never leaving out a review only because it's low.
